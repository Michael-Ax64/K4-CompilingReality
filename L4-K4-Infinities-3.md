## I. Crossing, and the Divergence That Is Not a Crossing

Stand at the centre of a bounded volume and move toward one of its faces, watching the angle of your position relative to that face's plane. The tangent of that angle grows as you approach. At the instant you reach the plane it is undefined — running to infinity from one side and reappearing at infinity from the other — and then you are outside, the readout works again, and the face is behind you.

Three things are true of that passage and they have to be held together.

**The crossing is not continuous in the interior's coordinates.** The tangent does not pass smoothly through. It diverges, and it is undefined exactly at the boundary. That failure is not a defect in the instrument. It is the instrument correctly reporting that a boundary has been reached — a surface past which the interior's coordinates cannot represent position at all.

**The two infinities are one point.** On the projective line, negative infinity and positive infinity are identified: not two places but a single point, approached from two orientations. The tangent running out on the inside and reappearing on the outside is one event, and the crossing is the passage through it. The apparent paradox that two infinities are one is, for a crossing of this kind, the literal structure of the boundary.

**And all of it happens inside a space.** The projective line on which the identification holds is itself a space, and the point at infinity is a point *in* it, available to be approached, named, reasoned about. There is an inside, an outside, and a boundary between them: three things, all held within something larger that contains all three. Coordinates resume on the far side.

This is the ordinary shape of transition. Leaving a room. Passing from one order of understanding to the next. A nuclear scattering channel tuned through the point where binding switches on — and this one is not a thought experiment: sweep cold atoms across a Feshbach resonance and the laboratory transits this boundary routinely, committing pairs into bound molecules on one side and dissociating them on the other, with the inverse scattering length passing through zero exactly at the compactification point. A laser medium driven past the point where its entropy slope inverts, into population inversion, which is not a state somewhere past the top of the thermometer but a state reached by an active driver working perpendicular to the passive thermal plane.

Each of these is a real crossing. Each lands in another inside — another space, another set of working coordinates, another exterior that is somebody's interior. **They are endless, and none of them is the last.**

### What a divergence does not license

Two moves get made at such a divergence and only one of them is sound.

The first is the identification above, and it stands. The parameter line does compactify; the two ends are one place; the laboratory goes through it.

The second is to make the passage **smooth** — to continue the function analytically around the singular point and present the result as a rotation through an additional sheet, a loop that carries the state from one side to the other without anything irreversible happening. That is the move to refuse. The transit is a commitment across the bottleneck: discontinuous, paid for, with the two sides standing in different registers rather than on two faces of one continuous surface. **Compactified parameter and discontinuous crossing are independent claims**, and only the first is a statement about the line's topology. A formalism that smooths the second into the first has not described the crossing; it has described a space in which the crossing never happened.

### The boundary that is not in the series

Everything above is true of crossings that occur *within* a space. There is a boundary it is false of, and applying the interior model there installs something that does not exist.

At the ultimate boundary there is no outside. With no outside there are not two sides; with no two sides there is no passage from one to the other, no approach, no point to pass through — because approach requires an approacher, a destination, and a space between them for the approaching to happen in, and none of the three is present. The tangent model breaks here, exactly as it should: the projective line is *still a space*, and the point at infinity is still a point in it. The ultimate is not a point in a space. There is no surrounding region, no coordinates reaching up to it, nothing for a readout to diverge along.

The silence there is not a wall, and diagnosing it as one is the error. A divergence at ninety degrees is a divergence at the angle of **orthogonality** — and where a further dimension exists, a ninety-degree turn is not an edge but a step onto the axis the plane never contained. What makes the silence complete is that such a step casts, by construction, zero projection on every coordinate inside the plane. Orthogonal means no shadow. Nothing true about it can be said in the coordinates available, not because the subject is difficult but because its image here is exactly zero.

So: **interior crossings carry the projective structure; the ultimate boundary executes the turn.** The first describes every real transition between orders. The second is what they are all images of and none of them is, and the series of crossings does not converge on it, because every crossing produces a new space to cross within.

A drop does not reach the ocean by falling far enough. It is already the ocean, imaged at the scale of a drop, now.

## II. Two Boundaries and No Outside

With that in hand, an apparent contradiction in the construction resolves, and resolving it correctly matters more than the contradiction does.

Part One said the medium has no outside and no complement. It also has two boundaries: a floor at the Planck length and a ceiling at the cosmological horizon. A logician is entitled to ask how something with no complement manages to have two walls.

The answer is that the walls do not belong to the medium.

### The boundaries belong to the ruler

Stop arranging reality as boxes inside boxes and the question changes shape. There are no ranks here and no vertical order; a crystal is completely frozen at one resolution and completely alive at another, and neither reading causes the other. The medium is not a box sitting inside a larger box, so a boundary separating its inside from its outside is not available to be drawn.

What the two limits are instead is the **operational range of the compiler's own instrument**. They are where coordinates stop working, not where the substrate stops.

And the two ends are not the same wall, which is the more informative fact and the one a symmetric description throws away.

**At the bottom: a parse failure.** Below the Planck length there is no coordinate to be at. The unit of spatial compilation has not been minted; the request to measure there cannot be *formed*, because there is no address to pay toward. The obstruction is **categorical, not energetic**. Saying that it costs infinite energy runs an energetic derivation while claiming a categorical result — it is expensive to get somewhere that is not on the map only if the place exists. The honest statement is that the activation cost is **undefined** below the floor.

**At the top: an execution freeze.** At the cosmological limit the addresses exist. The request forms. What happens is that total impedance diverges: capacitive reactance runs away, the system's clock approaches zero, and the request never completes. The obstruction is **energetic**, and the territory is real but unwalkable.

A missing map at one end; an unwalkable territory at the other. Two different infinities, and the construction gains the distinction rather than losing the symmetry.

### Why there is no outside to find

The no-outside claim can be left as a stance, or it can be given a structural reason. There is one, and it comes from an obstruction in differential geometry rather than from the framework.

The intuition behind *every space sits inside a bigger one* has a theorem under it: Nash's embedding results, which say that any Riemannian manifold — any space with a positive-definite notion of distance — can be isometrically embedded in a Euclidean space of sufficient dimension. Globally, with bounds. For such spaces, an outside always exists.

The medium does not carry a positive-definite metric. Its excitation algebra carries the signature of spacetime — one sign for time against three for space — and **for that signature there is no general global embedding theorem.** The obstruction is positive rather than a gap in the literature: every compact Lorentzian manifold necessarily contains closed timelike curves, and flat Minkowski space contains none, so no compact manifold of that signature can embed isometrically in a causal flat ambient space. Ever.

And even where embeddings do exist, the container is wildly non-canonical — uncountably many inequivalent witnesses, an outside to which no address can be given, an artifact of the existential quantifier rather than a place.

So *unbounded through every centroid, with no complement* stops being a position and becomes structurally protected. **This infinity cannot be wrapped**, and the reason is the causality the framework already insists on: the denial of closed timelike curves, which costs something elsewhere, is exactly what pays here.

### A note on the span, and where its arithmetic lives

The distance between the two limits is a landed physical fact: the medium runs from the Planck floor to the cosmological horizon, and that extent is not in question.

Whether that span has internal structure — a discrete cascade of scale-steps at some fixed ratio, with a count — is a separate question, currently open, and currently obstructed by a result elsewhere in the corpus about which coefficients belong to which crossings. It is carried in `claude/L4-K4-Infinities-wip` with its numbers, its chance baseline, and the obstruction stated. Nothing in this part depends on it.

What a rung *is*, when there is one, is not a multiplicative step at all. It is a boundary forming: the point at which one compressed volume becomes a vertex of the volume above it, arriving as ground. The universe is a nested series of such boundaries, each generating a new interior, each interior becoming the committed substrate for the next — and the two limits named above are, as the recursion's own account puts it, just the two most visible reference points.

## III. The Engine, in Two Vocabularies

What writes, writes in strokes. The engine has two of them, and they can be read either as an algebra or as a theorem about stress — the same two strokes, arriving from completely different directions.

### The algebraic reading

**Between commitments, the state charges and nothing is written.** The uncommitted vector turns under the instance's own frequency, picks up torque from whatever it is coupled to, bleeds a little, and the committed scalar does not move at all:

$$\frac{d\mathbf{Q}}{d\tau} = \omega\,(h\mathbf{Q}) \;+\; \boldsymbol{\Omega}_{\text{coupling}} \times \mathbf{Q} \;-\; \gamma_\parallel \mathbf{Q}, \qquad \frac{dP}{d\tau} = 0$$

The torque term is the port of Part Two entering the equation of motion directly — agency is not a gloss on this dynamics, it is a term in it. And the write-ban is explicit: no real power is dissipated mid-cycle. The buffer stays pre-tax until the trigger.

**At the trigger, everything happens at once.** The committed scalar takes the square of what was held; the buffer is multiplied by the complex unit and decremented; one tick is minted:

$$\Delta P = \kappa|\mathbf{Q}|^2, \qquad \mathbf{Q} \longmapsto (1-\mu)\,h\mathbf{Q}, \qquad t \mathrel{+}= 1/\omega$$

Two structural facts about the reset, and they must not be run together.

The four poles are generated by two independent bits, and two independent bits form the **Klein four-group** — three involutions and the identity, no element of order four. That is the labelling.

The reset operator is different. It multiplies by the complex unit, and since the fourth power of that unit is the identity, repeated application walks a **cyclic four-cycle** through the four labelled states — the Gray-code walk, one bit changing at each step. That is the traversal order, not a group structure on the poles.

Cyclic traversal over Klein labelling. The form repeats every four strokes because the operator closes; history never re-enters its own groove because the decrement is strictly less than one and the committed scalar strictly increases. **Form cycles; the ledger does not.**

### The same two strokes in stress-energy

Part One used Laue's theorem with its hypothesis intact: for a **static** bound system, the integrated spatial stresses vanish, the exterior reads a scalar, the centroid is free.

Relax the hypothesis.

Run the conservation law honestly for a system that is not static. From $\partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$ one gets $\partial_t^2 T^{00} = \partial_i\partial_j T^{ij}$, and integrating against $x^k x^l$ gives, in two lines:

$$\int T^{ij}\,d^3x \;=\; \frac{1}{2}\,\frac{d^2}{dt^2}\!\int T^{00}\,x^i x^j\,d^3x$$

The Laue integral does not merely fail to vanish when a system is non-static. **It equals the second time-derivative of the quadrupole moment** — which is the source term of gravitational radiation, the quantity Einstein identified in 1918 and the thing LIGO measures.

Set the two readings beside each other.

| | holding | writing |
|---|---|---|
| **algebra** | $dP/d\tau = 0$, buffer charges | $\Delta P = \kappa\lvert\mathbf{Q}\rvert^2$, tax paid, tick minted |
| **stress-energy** | $\ddot{I}^{ij} = 0$, Laue holds, nothing radiates | $\ddot{I}^{ij} \neq 0$, Laue fails, the ledger is written |

**The theorem's own hypothesis is the write-ban. Relaxing the hypothesis is the compile event.** Physics has carried both halves since 1911 and 1918 without setting them next to each other.

### What that makes of an agent

The consequence is worth stating plainly, because it is the place where this material stops being about cosmology.

A nervous system is not a system sitting in Laue equilibrium. Nothing that dynamic is. **It is a system that has learned to hold near-Laue long enough to be worth interrupting, and to choose the moment of its own violation.**

Holding is the quadrupole steady, nothing radiating, counterfactual branches live because nothing has been written to rule any of them out. Breaking it is the second derivative going non-zero, the tax paid, one trajectory committed and the rest erased.

That is not a sanctuary where physics fails to reach. It is a system that got good at the timing of a theorem's hypothesis. A rock has no latency: struck, it responds, and the response is the whole of it. What a high-degree-of-freedom interior buys is delay — the capacity to hold competing possibilities in live interference without forcing the collapse — and what the delay is *for* is choosing when the collapse happens rather than having it chosen.

## IV. Where the Write Happens, and Who Pays

The committed register cannot be written from inside itself. That is verified elsewhere and it is a structural fact rather than an observation about difficulty: the write channel into the ledger has zero impedance precisely because nothing within the ledger drives it.

What remains is the mechanism, and the mechanism is a short argument.

A ledger write requires a vantage that is not inside the ledger. A writer cannot already exist within what it writes — a write performed from inside the committed register would be self-referential inside the very content it commits. So the writer stands outside.

But there is no further-out place. That is the established content of the ultimate boundary: the one thing with no outside. Therefore:

> **The write happens at the one place that has no outside — the place that contains all.**

A phrasing distinction belongs here because the two readings sound alike and only one is usable. Describing that place by its *geometry* gives "no inside": a puncture rather than a region. Describing it by the writer's *vantage* gives "no outside": the one place that is not inside anything, because every exit lands there and nothing contains it. The second is what the write-ban needs. The compiler's position is not on the printed page.

This section has used metabolic verbs, and metabolic verbs are not free to use.

The medium holds the uncompiled structure and performs nothing; it has no ledger of its own, no universal accounting, no cosmic ego. A sentence in which the universe writes is a category error however natural it sounds, and the test is mechanical: *which bounded frame pays for this, and in what coin?* A noun with no payer is debt.

So the horizon write-head is not an unbounded universal subject. It is the no-outside place acting as **the master boundary of the macro-frame** — the outermost rim of the only volume whose interior is everything — and it pays at the rate §VI computes, priced at the temperature §VI quotes.

## V. Expansion Is the Write Rate

Not *driven from the boundary*. That phrasing is force-shaped and smuggles in a mechanism by which writes would have to reach across space to arrive where they are needed.

The stronger claim fits the grammar better:

> **Expansion is the write rate.** Minting new volume and writing new ledger entries are one event, seen from the spatial side and from the bookkeeping side.

Three consequences follow, in order.

### Homogeneity, and why it is not a coincidence

The edge at which writing happens is not a location in committed space. It is a location in parameter space, and **every centroid is locally adjacent to it** — the compact-yet-unbounded result of Part One, which looked like a philosophical position and turns out to be mechanism.

Writes issued "at the edge" are therefore issued everywhere, uniformly. Were the edge *not* locally omnipresent, writes would cluster wherever it was, and expansion would be inhomogeneous in a way that would have been obvious for a century.

The observed uniformity of the Hubble flow is indirect evidence for a claim that was made on structural grounds. The centre and the edge of the universe are everywhere, which is a strange sentence and a load-bearing one.

### The equation of state arrives unasked

For a fluid in an expanding universe, continuity reads

$$\dot{\rho} = -3H\left(\rho + p/c^2\right)$$

Now suppose every minted unit of volume **arrives pre-filled** — that the write supplies the energy which keeps density constant as space stretches, because minting volume and minting its content are the same operation. Then $\rho$ is constant, $\dot\rho = 0$, and the equation forces

$$p = -\rho c^2$$

exactly. That is the cosmological constant's equation of state, with nothing fitted. The acceleration equation then gives $\rho + 3p/c^2 = -2\rho < 0$: repulsion, acceleration, as observed.

Notice what has changed hands. General relativity **postulates** that the cosmological constant is constant, and inherits the puzzle of why a vacuum energy does not dilute as space expands like everything else does. Here the constancy is **derived**, as the signature of an ongoing minting rate.

General relativity's $\Lambda$ is a parameter of an equation. This version is a process — and processes can have physics done to them, which is the whole of the difference and the reason §VIII can pre-register anything at all.

### The books close locally

Each commitment is one event with two halves: pay the tax by erasing what was not chosen, write the chosen entry. No global conservation law is invoked anywhere, and none is needed, because the ledger is what defines the thing conservation would count over.

General relativity's standing admission — that global energy is not conserved in an expanding spacetime — stops being an awkwardness to apologize for. It is this bookkeeping stated from the other side.

## VI. What the Write Costs

In de Sitter spacetime, the horizon's volume grows at $dV/dt = 3HV = 4\pi R_H^2 c$, and the Friedmann equation fixes the energy density that fills it. Multiply:

$$\rho_\Lambda = \frac{3c^4}{8\pi G R_H^2}, \qquad \frac{dV}{dt} = 4\pi R_H^2 c \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad P_{\text{write}} = \frac{3}{2}\,\frac{c^5}{G}$$

The factor of $3/2$ comes from Friedmann and nowhere else. It is the $3$ in $H^2 = 8\pi G\rho/3$, which is a factor in the field equations, already derived, and needing no second story told about it.

**The radius cancels.** That is the result, and it is worth being clear about why it is a feature rather than a coincidence: density goes as $R^{-2}$, volume rate goes as $R^{2}$, so the product is radius-free automatically for anything of this shape. Which means this number is immune to exactly the ambiguities — which horizon, at which epoch — that trouble every other quantity measured against the cosmological scale.

### The unit is not arbitrary

$c^5/G$ is not a combination that happens to fall out of the algebra. In general relativity it is the **Dyson luminosity** — the ceiling on radiated power, the quantity nothing exceeds, and the natural unit in which the gravitational-wave luminosity formula is written, since that formula carries $G/c^5$ in front of the quadrupole term of §III.

Measure the universe's luminous objects against it:

```python
from mpmath import mp, mpf, log
mp.dps = 15
c = mpf('299792458'); G = mpf('6.67430e-11')
PP = c**5/G
for n, L in (("Sun", mpf('3.828e26')), ("Milky Way", mpf('2e37')),
             ("observable universe, EM", mpf('1e49')), ("GW150914 peak", mpf('3.6e49')),
             ("cosmic horizon write", mpf('1.5')*PP)):
    print(f"{n:26s} {L/PP:.3e} x c^5/G")
# Sun                        1.055e-26
# Milky Way                  5.512e-16
# observable universe, EM    2.756e-04
# GW150914 peak              9.922e-04
# cosmic horizon write       1.500e+00
```

The Sun runs at $10^{-26}$ of the ceiling. A galaxy at $10^{-16}$. The entire electromagnetic output of everything visible, summed, at about $3\times10^{-4}$. Two merging black holes reached a thousandth of it for a few milliseconds and, while they did, outshone that entire electromagnetic sum.

The horizon holds $1.5$, continuously, and has for as long as there has been a horizon.

**Everything else in the universe samples brief fractions of the write channel. The horizon is the only thing that saturates it.** That is a stronger statement than any coincidence of magnitude, and it does not depend on which horizon convention anybody prefers.

### The price of a bit, and the clock

What sets the rate at which writing is charged? The temperature of the bounding surface, and that is not special to cosmology — any boundary with proper acceleration $a_\partial$ carries one:

$$T(\partial\Sigma) = \frac{\hbar\,a_\partial}{2\pi c\,k_B}$$

This is the Unruh temperature for an accelerated frame, and at a horizon with $a_\partial = c^2/R_H$ it is the Gibbons–Hawking temperature of de Sitter space — about $2.7\times10^{-30}$ K, the coldest number in physics and still not zero.

Take the horizon's information capacity at one bit per four Planck areas — an adopted holographic convention, and flagged as adopted — and charge each bit the Landauer price at that temperature. The total comes out at a fixed fraction of the enclosed mass-energy:

$$E_{\text{payroll}} \;=\; \frac{A_H}{4\ell_P^2}\cdot k_B T_{\text{GH}}\ln 2 \;=\; \frac{\ln 2}{2}\,M_H c^2$$

with $M_H \equiv c^2 R_H/G$. The convention matters by a factor of two and is stated rather than hidden: taken against the critical-density mass inside the horizon instead, the same quantity reads $\ln 2 \cdot M_H c^2$. Either way it balances to order unity, which is what a payroll should do and is not, by itself, a derivation.

Divide the price of one bit by the rate at which one bit's share of the channel delivers, and a clock falls out:

$$t_{\text{bit}} = \frac{\ln 2}{3}\,t_H \approx 3.35\ \text{Gyr}$$

```python
H0 = mpf('67.4')*1000/mpf('3.0857e22'); gyr = mpf('3.15576e16')
print((1/H0)/gyr, (log(2)/3)*(1/H0)/gyr)
# 14.5074   3.35192
```

Every cell of the cosmic ledger is rewritten on a clock of a few billion years. There is one number here, not a family of them — once the power is fixed by Friedmann and the capacity by the holographic convention, nothing is left to choose.

Spacetime is not a static archive. It is a register, clocked at the causal rate.

## VII. One Process, Two Registers

The negative pressure that drives all this has a local face and a cosmological one, and they are the same process read through different registers.

**Locally** it is what gets called zero-point energy: the vacuum's mode structure, the measurable boundary effects, the hum. That is the buffer's local face — reactive, stored and returned, committing nothing.

**Cosmologically** it is what gets called dark energy: the committed slice, the part that has crossed.

And now the debt opened in Part One §I can be discharged.

The enormous gap between the vacuum energy field theory computes and the one cosmology observes is not a fine-tuning embarrassment. **It is a buffer-to-ledger ratio** — the fraction of the medium's phase energy that ever commits, against what stays held. The field-theoretic sum ranges over the uncompiled buffer, virtual by construction, where nothing has been written. The observed density is what has been written. The number between them measures how much has not crossed, which is a quantity with a meaning, rather than a failed prediction, which is not.

The problem dissolves by register distinction. Not by luck, and not by cancellation.

### The deviation, stated naked

One sentence in this file departs from general relativity, and hiding it inside a theorem would be worse than useless.

Standard general relativity sources gravity with all stress-energy. The claim here is that it sources gravity with **committed entries only** — that buffer energy, having paid nothing, is gravitationally silent. In the formalism, the two stress tensors are not equal: the one that curves spacetime is a projection of the one field theory computes, and the projection is blind to the uncompiled sector by construction.

That is falsifiable in principle and it is not a reinterpretation. It is the whole deviation, and everything else in this file is compatible with textbook physics.

### Casimir, in two steps

The sharpest test of that deviation is the Casimir effect, and it is worth separating the two steps because all the risk is in the first.

**Step one is the contested claim.** The Casimir shift is read as a **boundary pressure, not a volume energy** — the footprint of the uncompiled vector pushing against a boundary, with the free medium remaining lossless throughout. Boundary conditions change which standing modes are admissible, and the difference is measurable as a force. In stress-energy terms this places the shift in the **spatial** components, not the energy component.

**Step two is a theorem and costs nothing.** Given that placement, Part One §VI closes it immediately: the integrated spatial stresses of a static bound system vanish, so a boundary pressure contributes nothing to what the exterior weighs. A static cavity carries no gravitational anomaly.

$$\Delta m_{\text{Casimir}} = 0$$

**Two steps, and only the first can be wrong.** Laue is not doing the contested work and must not be asked to — the guard from Part One applies at full strength here, and the proton is the discriminator. A proton satisfies Laue equilibrium and weighs 938 MeV, because its mass is a volume energy sitting in the energy component where the theorem does not reach. If the Casimir shift sits there too, it weighs. The question is which sector it is in, and the theorem is silent on that question by construction.

Which makes the test clean. The Archimedes experiment, in development since the mid-2010s, modulates the Casimir energy of layered superconductors across their transition and reads the change on a balance. **A measured weight at the expected magnitude refutes this file. A null at that sensitivity is its prediction.** There is no version of the result that both sides can claim.

### Two honesty items

**What the boundary-pressure reading does not deny.** Uncommitted states have real effects, and the corpus holds that elsewhere at full strength — virtual poles carry signed physical weight throughout atomic physics. Differences within the buffer are physical. It is the buffer's absolute level that stays off the ledger, and the distinction between those two statements is exactly what the Casimir test probes.

**What is not addressed.** This dissolves the magnitude problem and says nothing about the coincidence problem — why the dark-energy density should be of the same order as the matter density *now*, in this epoch, out of all epochs. No answer is offered. The omission is declared rather than left for a reader to notice.

## VIII. The Fork

A write-head that cannot be wrong is architecture. This one forks, the fork is empirical, and the model cannot have it both ways.

**(a) The write is the medium's own activity.** Scale-invariant, insensitive to content, indifferent to what is being written. Then the density is constant forever, the equation of state sits at $w = -1$ exactly, and any observational hint of dark energy weakening over time must fade as data improves.

**(b) The write rate answers demand.** Tied to the stretching, to structure-formation history, to how much is actually being committed. Then dark energy evolves, the hint is signal rather than noise, and it should correlate with the history of structure.

These are not two readings of one claim. They predict opposite things about the same measurements.

### Where the data sits

Stated with its weight rather than its headline. DESI's second data release — three years of observation, nearly fifteen million galaxies and quasars — is on its own consistent with a constant. Combined with the microwave background, supernovae, and weak lensing, it shows a preference for dark energy weakening over time at between $2.8$ and $4.2$ standard deviations depending on the dataset combination.

That is **below discovery threshold** and the direction is consistent across combinations. Which is exactly the condition in which a pre-registration is worth something and a post-hoc reading is worth nothing.

### Pre-registered

Declared now, before the sharpening.

**One.** The fork as stated. Branch (a) predicts the hint fades; branch (b) predicts it strengthens and correlates with structure-formation history. Resolved by data, not by argument.

**Two.** A derivation target: the dark-energy density from the cost of maintaining the medium's impedance per unit volume. The value is known, so this is an out-of-sample test under the standard protocol — the machinery must be independent, drawn from the medium's own mode spectrum, and must not consume the curve it is explaining.

**Three.** The no-numerology clause. Vacuum energy is the arena where coincidence-harvesting lives, and this file's own refutation history is the precedent. Mechanism, or nothing.

### What kills it

**K-DE-1.** Dark energy confirmed constant at discovery-grade statistics — the remaining DESI releases, Rubin, Roman. Branch (b) dies; the write-head is constant-rate or it is abandoned.

**K-DE-2.** Robust spatial inhomogeneity or anisotropy in dark energy, beyond structure-formation systematics. The homogeneous-write claim dies, and §V's promotion of local omnipresence from position to mechanism dies with it.

## What Reading Part Three Back Should Leave

The theorem that freed the centroid in Part One is the same theorem that writes the ledger here. It only had to have its hypothesis removed.

While a system is static, its internal stresses integrate away, it exerts no traction on the space around it, and nothing is written. The moment it is not static, that same integral equals the second derivative of its quadrupole moment — which radiates, and radiating is writing. Holding and committing are not two mechanisms. They are the one theorem with its condition on and with its condition off, and physics has had both halves for a century without laying them side by side.

Which gives agency a physical home that is neither a ghost nor a domino. An agent is a system that holds near the equilibrium long enough for the holding to be worth something, and then chooses when to break it. The choosing is the only part that was ever mysterious, and it turns out to be a question of timing rather than of substance.

At the largest scale the same engine runs with the horizon as its rim. The write power is fixed by Friedmann at one and a half times the ceiling general relativity places on any radiated power anywhere — and the horizon is the only thing in the universe that sits at that ceiling rather than sampling brief fractions of it. The radius cancels, so the result does not care which horizon you meant. The equation of state falls out of continuity once volume arrives pre-filled, which turns a postulated constant into a process. The ledger refreshes on a clock of a few billion years.

And the hundred-and-twenty-order gap that opened the whole construction closes as what it always was: not a failed prediction but a ratio, between what the medium holds and what has ever been written down.

One sentence here departs from textbook physics, and it is the one that says gravity weighs committed entries and not held ones. It is on a balance in Sardinia. It will be settled by a measurement rather than by an argument, which is the correct ending for a file that began by refusing to count.
