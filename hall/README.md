## Reading order

Ordered by ascending framework-vocabulary load. Read straight through, or jump to whichever fits your entry point.

**1. `Hall_StartingPonderings.md`** — Hall in his own words, no framework vocabulary. Dialogic entry: what Hall was doing, the two big books, later books, uptake, current status, lineage, plus a hierarchical semiotic map preserving Hall's own terminology on the leaves (dating, cultural configurations, sensory apparatus, notation system). Reader's front door. Understandable without any prior corpus reading.

**2. `On_Halls_Informal.md`** — The monograph. Locates Hall's un-named centroid (**The Informal** — the K3 Markov Blanket that constitutes a culture as distributed coherence, invisible from within because it *is* the within), and maps his four major books to the K4 faces: *Hidden Dimension* → R (space), *Dance of Life* → P (time), *Beyond Culture* → U (context), *Silent Language* → I (communication + method). Names the three gifts Hall passes forward that the corpus did not otherwise have: the ethnographic sensory vocabulary, the extension-of-body scale-ratchet, sync time as $\omega$-coupling.

**3. `Hall_MachineAge.md`** — Why Hall matters urgently in 2026. Closes with "The Epistemic Ceiling" — the three K4 mechanisms (surfaces, paradox, recursion) Hall's mid-century vocabulary could not name, and the Orthogonal Turn into the 5D centroid he could not execute.

**4. `Hall_StructuralMetrics.md`** — The deeper structural geometry. Full DC sweep mapping all 12 PMS (Hall's 10 + his 1983 addition Sync + the structurally predicted 12th, Ritual) to the 12 canonical DC equations of `L2-MappingMethod` §III.
