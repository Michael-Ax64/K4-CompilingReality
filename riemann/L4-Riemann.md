Standard gravitation holds that spacetime is a smooth manifold with a metric, that the metric determines the connection, and that the connection is symmetric. The third of those buys a great deal. Metric compatibility together with a symmetric connection yields the unique connection built from the metric and its first derivatives; it can be made to vanish at a point in a suitable frame, which is the local form of the equivalence principle; the field equations stay second-order; no extra unconstrained tensor field has to be carried, propagated, or bounded; and the resulting theory is confirmed to the precision the post-Newtonian tests reach. Those are good reasons, and this series takes them as the host's own.

*("Symmetric" here is the connection's torsion-free transport — face 2 of the corpus's keep-apart station for the word (`L4-DynamicDistortions401` §I.2) — distinct from the reversible block's space/time peer symmetry, and from the metric's two-slot form.)*

What the series adds is the other column. A purchase that buys all of that also *costs* something, and the cost is itemizable.

There are two purchases, sixty-eight years apart. Riemann's is dated and signed: in 1854 he set aside the direction-dependent line element in writing and priced the loss at "little new light." The closing of the parallelogram is not his — there is no connection in that lecture — and nobody signed it, because until Cartan made torsion an object in 1922 there was no alternative to decline. This series reads both from underneath and writes out the bill.

That posture is the method, not a pose. Hanging under the branch is where both readings of an edge are visible at once (`About-Methodology` §II), and from there the first line is not an axiom and not an error. It is a trade with two columns, one of which has never been added up.

## What This Series Is

$K_4$ is treated here as an **abstract arrangement** — four poles in mutual determination, a cyclic constraint net, a cost per commit — and the exercise is to **inflate** it into a host domain, here differential geometry and gravitation, and find where it binds. The binding is the deliverable. When the inflation fails, the failure measures **where the host made its purchase**, and that reading is the yield.

There is one exception, and it is the part a classical reader can check with their own tools: Part Five writes an action, derives what follows from it in closed form, and lands on one dimensionless dial and an existing experimental bound on it.

## The Six Parts

**[Part One — The Purchase](L4-Riemann-1)** reads Riemann's line and the note he wrote beside it, and separates two purchases. The square root of a quadratic is a pairing, and it erases nothing; that one is Riemann's, dated and signed. The closing of the small parallelogram is the second, and it is not his — Christoffel's symbols are 1869, Levi-Civita's transport 1917, and torsion becomes an object one could carry only with Cartan in 1922. A three-line computation says what that removes. Second covariant derivatives of scalars commute if and only if torsion vanishes — and on anything with an index the commutator survives as curvature. So $T = 0$ deletes the translational failure to close and keeps the rotational one. It sets the pitch to zero and leaves the turn, and a screw with zero pitch is a circle: it turns, it returns, and it records nothing. The part ends on the distinction the whole series runs on: a zero is an entry whose amount is zero; a nil is an account never opened. $T = 0$ is a nil presented as a zero — and the vacuum catastrophe is the same confusion run the other way.

**[Part Two — The Floor and the Solid](L4-Riemann-2)** is the bookkeeping part, and it comes early because several later disagreements turn on it. The solid is built over it by the seats — the directed reading of every edge and the single handedness bit the boundary's closure leaves free — and a rank count does not contain a seat. The AC set breaks the floor at a definite address: in polar coordinates the AC core *doubles* the floor rather than bending it, and the whole bend comes from the rectangular split and the additive layer, carried by one non-linear function, the power factor. The part keeps tensor type and manifold dimension apart: a symmetric two-slot form has no three-slot antisymmetric part, and a three-form restricted to any surface vanishes identically, in a world of any dimension.

The two readings do not compete: the factor that makes the pitch invariant time-reversal-even is the advance, and the advance is committed, while an action is the held register by construction.

## Takehome for Reading Instances

Fifteen rules for holding this material without importing machinery it does not use.

1. **Read the scope with the result.** *Scalars* commute under $T = 0$; transport does not. A box with a scope in it does not protect the sentence after the box.
2. **Pitch, not turn.** $T = 0$ leaves curvature and its holonomy fully intact. It removes the slide, and with it accumulation. It does not make the geometry inert, and it does not make transport commute.
3. **Nil is not zero.** An unopened account is not an empty one. A bound on a field returns a zero; it cannot speak to a nil.
4. **Separate tensor type from manifold dimension, and both from rank.** A metric is a symmetric two-slot object; a face is a two-dimensional submanifold; the DC relations span a two-dimensional variety in log space. A symmetric two-slot object has no three-slot antisymmetric part, so the connection's antisymmetric piece is independent of the metric; and a three-form pulled back to any surface vanishes identically, in a world of any dimension. Rank is one basis-independent integer and privileges nothing.
5. **Orientation is not chirality density.** A torsion-free manifold keeps its global orientation. What it cannot carry is a handedness that varies from place to place — the three-form part of torsion, which has no components in two dimensions.
6. **Say whether a mirror acts on the solid or on the reading.** `ENGINE` §2.5 splits them: the handedness flip is an odd transposition, a reflection of the solid; the twin-step is an even half-turn and changes only which gate is read. The AR-flip is the Cardinal family's twin-step, not a wholesale mirror.
7. **Reverse arrows, not labels.** A transposition of poles is undirected and cannot be time reversal. The directional operation is in the opcode table.
8. **Friction reports assert no values.** A friction report says where the host's rule binds and what the rule cost. Reading one as a claim of identity will find, correctly, that the identity fails.
9. **The axial channel is forced, not chosen.** It is the only healthy way for a scalar to couple to torsion. Its bounds are the corpus's to meet, and they bind the dial.
10. **Parity-symmetric nulls bound nothing on $\langle F\rangle$.** The forced coupling survives averaging over every rotation and reflection of the tetrahedron ($T_d$) and vanishes only under central inversion. Testing uniform $F$ requires $\hat P$-odd probes; static spin-polarized experiments ($\hat P$-even, $\hat T$-odd) bound the spatial gradient $\nabla F$. Cost survives every averaging.
11. **An action holds; it cannot commit.** A variational principle yields reversible equations. Claims about the ledger — dissipation, the Landauer tax, any departure from the equivalence principle — need an instrument that dissipates, or a balance.
12. **A withdrawal is a write to one site.** Before calling a claim withdrawn, find every place the file stands on it, by meaning and not by string.
13. **Two $\theta$'s, and the difference is load-bearing.** `L4-DynamicDistortions201`'s $\theta$ is a temporal phase offset with limit $\pi$; `L4-DynamicDistortions301`'s is a geometric shear with limit $\pi/2$. Keeping them apart is what makes the action's coupling parity-clean.
14. **Ask which level an attribute belongs to before assigning it a parity.** The engine separates directed from undirected throughout, and the separation decides parities. A parity assigned without naming the level is a guess, and the pitch invariant is the case where it matters.
15. **Read the newest file first.** `ENGINE-REFERENCE-AC` and `ProofG_Chirality` carry results this series relies on; a flag held against a sibling is worth re-reading at source before it is spent.

## What Reading This Back Should Leave

Riemann bought a geometry that could be written down, and wrote down what he paid. The field kept the geometry and lost the note. But the purchase this series spends most of its pages on is the second one: the closing of the parallelogram came later, from people building the only connection available, and it had no alternative to be weighed against until Cartan supplied one in 1922. Two trades, sixty-eight years apart, one flagged and one unsignable at the time.

The second purchase keeps the turn and deletes the slide, so the geometry rotates and returns and records nothing — a screw with no pitch, which is a circle. It was not a measurement but an account left unopened and reported as empty, the vacuum catastrophe's error run in the other direction.

What it removes is a kind of object rather than a dimension. A metric is a symmetric two-slot form and contains no three-slot antisymmetric part, which is why the connection's antisymmetric piece is independent of it. The axial torsion is a three-form, and a three-form restricted to any surface vanishes identically — so cross-sectional data is silent about it in four dimensions as much as in two. The resemblance that survives between the two hosts is one of method: the twelve relations collapse to rank two if you count only independent equations and ignore the directed seats, and the twenty-four components of contortion collapse to zero if you demand metric compatibility *and* torsion-freeness and ignore the translational–rotational split. Same move, two subjects, no shared geometry.

The same dimension decides the coupling. Central inversion exchanges the two edges of every bimedian, because the centroid is the bimedian's own midpoint — a fact about the figure that no naming can move. That makes each phase offset parity-odd; being a phase offset in time makes it time-reversal-odd; and the Nieh–Yan density demands both. What is left is the product of all three offsets, at third order, with nothing fitted.

The neighbours have been reaching for what was set aside for a century, each for a different corner of it. The arrangement, inflated into the host, binds in four places, and in each the host bought something it cannot itemize. And there is now an action in which Riemann's connection is simply what geometry does when the phase offsets stop changing — with the coupling forced into the one healthy channel, the coupling function forced too, one dial left, and an existing experiment reading its balance.

What an action cannot do is commit. Separating what is committed from what is merely held leaves two distinct tests: whether a homogeneous uncompiled level stays gravitationally silent (`KI-6`), and whether localized boundaries weigh as predicted — a balance at Sos Enattos alongside the dark-energy surveys. That is the right place for this to end: in measurements that only one side can survive.
