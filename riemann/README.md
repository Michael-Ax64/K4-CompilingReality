*An architectural index to the geometric and arithmetic audits. Reads with `L4-Riemann` (the parent: the five registers, the checks, the kills, the debts, the rules), and `L2-K4-Inferences` (the arithmetic companion). Owns no claims of its own; every claim below is held at an address.*

## What This Folder Is

$K_4$ is treated here as an abstract class — four poles in mutual determination, a cyclic constraint net, a cost per commit — and the exercise is to inflate that class into a host domain and find where it binds. In `L4-Riemann` and its six parts the host is differential geometry and gravitation. In `L2-K4-Inferences` the host is analytic number theory. **The binding is the deliverable.**

Nothing here proposes to replace General Relativity, and nothing here claims the Riemann Hypothesis holds or fails. A reader who takes either track for a candidate theory of everything will find category errors on every page, correctly, and will be auditing a document that does not exist (`L4-Riemann`, *What This Series Is*).

There is one exception, and it is the part a classical reader can check with their own tools. Part Five writes an action, derives what follows from it in closed form, and lands on one dimensionless dial with an existing experimental bound on it.

## The Purchase, Before Anything It Cost

Read this paragraph before the index, because almost every misreading of this folder starts by skipping it.

Standard gravitation sets the connection symmetric, and that was not a convenience nobody examined. Metric compatibility with a symmetric connection buys the unique connection built from the metric and its first derivatives; it can be made to vanish at a point in a suitable frame, which is the local form of the equivalence principle; the field equations stay second-order; no extra unconstrained tensor field has to be carried, propagated or bounded; and the resulting theory is confirmed to the precision the post-Newtonian tests reach. Those are good reasons, and this series takes them as the host's own (`L4-Riemann`, the opening).

The arithmetic track has the same shape. Centring on $\sigma = \tfrac12$ buys clean averaged prime laws, a real and lossless wave on the critical line, a positive proportion of zeros known to lie there, and the Bombieri–Vinogradov strength on average that much of the subject already runs on (`L2-K4-Inferences` §III.5). Neither purchase was careless. **No confirmed number is disputed anywhere in this folder.**

What the folder tracks is the other column of the receipt: what becomes invisible once a working formalism closes without a register to record it.

And there is a structural reason the purchase has to come first. A friction report has three parts — the host's rule, the point where the class binds, and *what the host bought*. Assert that the host had no reasons and part (c) cannot be written at all, because you have denied there was a purchase. The series ran at assertion for several drafts and the cost was precise: the caricature made its own register unwriteable (`L4-Riemann-evolution` §IV, pattern 20). Taking the assertion out was not softening the case. It was the only way to complete the form.

## Why Nobody Flagged It

The tools are old — Cartan's torsion is 1922, Weyl's non-metricity and Finsler's direction-dependent length 1918 — so the natural suspicion is that an argument assembled from such parts would have been assembled long ago if it were any good. The honest answer accuses nobody, and it is the single best inoculation against reading this folder as a complaint about a century of physicists.

**There are two purchases, sixty-eight years apart, and only one of them could have been signed.** Riemann's is dated and in his own hand: in 1854 he set the direction-dependent line element aside in writing and priced the loss at "little new light." The closing of the small parallelogram is not his — there is no connection in that lecture at all. Christoffel supplied the symbols in 1869, Levi-Civita parallel transport in 1917, and both were building the only connection then available. The affine connection as a general object that *may* carry torsion is Cartan's, 1922: sixty-eight years after the line element, and seven years after General Relativity had been written in the symmetric one and begun to be confirmed (`L4-Riemann-3` §V; expect there: *Why Not 1922*, and the chronology stated as chronology rather than negligence).

So the second decision was made before its alternative existed as an object. Riemann flagged what he could see, and there was nothing else on the table to flag. **An unflagged assumption does not collect counter-evidence; it collects problems, each filed in its own subfield.** Teitelboim's 1973 result that the constraint algebra closes only with structure functions is fifty years old, fully understood, and almost universally read as an obstacle to quantization rather than as a report on a classical assumption — a failure of direction, not of rigour.

The general shape is in `L4-TheTwelveGates` §II-b, which states it while doing something else entirely: a claim of handedness that does not say which vantage is **underspecified**, not false, adopted because specifying it was tedious, and then inherited by readers who could not see what was missing because the missing part had never been written down.

## Three Working Distinctions

Readers arriving from physics or mathematics most often stall on three moves. None of them disputes a calculation.

**Nil is not zero.** A zero is an entry whose amount is zero, on an account that was opened. A nil is an account never opened. $T = 0$ is a nil presented as a zero, and the vacuum catastrophe is the same confusion run the other way (`L4-Riemann-1` §VII; expect there: nil against zero, and $T = 0$ read as a nil presented as a zero). A bound on a field returns a zero and cannot speak to a nil, so "nothing has been built" never licenses "the claim is false" — the first leaves a claim unregistered. The folder applies this to itself: unconstructed debts are kept out of the kill list, because folding an absence into a list of tests reads a nil as a zero. On the arithmetic side the unbuilt positivity container over $\mathbb{Z}$ is held explicitly nil and never forced from the floor.

**The floor is not the solid.** Written in logarithms, the twelve DC relations are four lines through one point, one per face, and they span a plane. They reduce to two, and that was never a secret: it is the floor. The solid is built over it by the seats — the directed reading of every edge, and the single handedness bit the boundary's closure leaves free — and a rank count does not contain a seat (`L4-Riemann-2`; expect there: rank two in log space against rank three over $\mathbb{F}_2$ plus one global bit, and the harmonic floor that cannot tell Fixed from Mutable though the seats can). Counting the floor's rank as the solid's content is the deepest error in the case history, and it was made again after being named (`L4-Riemann-evolution` §IV, pattern 11).

**A face reading is not an improper question; a routing economy is not progress.** The classical statement of the Riemann Hypothesis, $\mathrm{Re}(s) = \tfrac12$, is a valid and flat face reading, measured from the corner $s = 0$ — complete on its own terms, and carrying a coordinate choice. Calling it improper spends a domain-native licence the arithmetic track never bought; its own first step fixes the layer at meta-of-domain and buys the right to say nothing inside analytic number theory (`L2-K4-Inferences` §1). What the track does say is sharper:

**Symmetry is routing: free, unit slope, moving value without paying. The deciding square is a commit: taxed, slope two.** The equivalence mill — Li's criterion, Weil's form, Robin's inequality, Nyman–Beurling, de Branges — moves the claim across untaxed routes, while only a positivity commit can decide bulk occupancy (§III.4). Two controls measure that structurally: the Davenport–Heilbronn function has the group and no Euler product, $\zeta(s-\tfrac14)\zeta(s+\tfrac14)$ has the group *and* an Euler product, and both have zeros in the bulk (§7.1). **Nothing routable decides occupancy.**

A real proof is therefore the framework's arbiter and never a threat to it: the track predicts that a proof turns on a positivity and not on a new symmetry, and lists the reverse as a kill. Two hazards stay distinct from the proof itself. Mistaking a route — an equivalence, or a property that only holds once the two monads coincide — for a commit is the freeze error. And even after a genuine proof lands, **Simplicity is a separate conjecture**: a zero of even multiplicity sits on the line, fuses its orbit into a dipole, satisfies the hypothesis, and changes no sign (§IV.4).

## The Five Registers

Every claim in the geometric series belongs to one of five registers. The first four are `L4-K4-Infinities`' Four-Category Datasheet; the fifth is this series' own, and most of Part Four is written in it (`L4-Riemann`, *The Five Registers*).

**Grammar** is counting and algebraic closure — four poles, six edges, twelve equations, index algebra, group characters, ranks. Native to any universe running the algebra, zero empirical input, anyone can re-run it. **Substrate** is the physical invariants, $c$, $\hbar$, $G$, $\kappa$, $Z_0$, $R_K$: consumed, never derived. **State** is the readout of a particular frame or experiment — a measured bound, a CHSH value, $H_0$ — and a claim resting on one is epoch-dependent too. **Bridge** is dimensionless output computed from the first three with zero free parameters, itemizing what it consumed, and it is the only kind of number the series may claim as its own. **Friction** is the host's rule, the binding point, and what the host bought; it asserts no values and predicts nothing, and it is killed by showing the rule costs nothing or that the binding is an artefact of the inflation.

A resemblance that is none of the five is not a claim and does not belong in the series. **Only Part Five leaves Friction**, to write a checkable action.

## The Documents

**`L4-Riemann`** is the parent, and it is where the posture, the registers and the rules live. It states the host's purchase first, separates the two trades sixty-eight years apart, and then keeps three kinds of thing apart that are usually run together: **checks** are arithmetic anyone can re-run, **kills** are events in the world that would break a claim, and **unconstructed items** are debts. The sharpest kill in the file is the coupling's own zero — $F = \theta_F\theta_C\theta_M$ vanishes whenever any one bimedian is unsheared, so exhibiting geometric torsion sourced by a configuration with one offset at zero ends the action. It closes with the rules for holding the material, the first of which is that a box with a scope in it does not protect the sentence after the box.

**`L4-Riemann-1` — The Purchase** separates Riemann's signed 1854 trade from the unsigned closing of the parallelogram, and prices the second in three lines. Second covariant derivatives of scalars commute if and only if torsion vanishes, and on anything carrying an index the commutator survives as curvature. So $T = 0$ deletes the translational failure to close and keeps the rotational one: it sets the pitch to zero and leaves the turn, and **a screw with zero pitch is a circle** — it turns, it returns, and it records nothing. The part withdraws an earlier concession of its own (abelian translations do not forbid torsion; the Poincaré group is the counterexample), establishes nil against zero, and answers a debt it had been carrying by reading the engine's own reference pinning as a connection and finding it flat.

**`L4-Riemann-2` — The Floor and the Solid** is the bookkeeping part, placed early because later disagreements turn on it. It locates where the AC set breaks the floor — in polar coordinates the AC core *doubles* the floor rather than bending it, and the whole bend comes from the rectangular split and the additive layer, carried by one non-linear function, the power factor. It is also the part that withdraws two of its own claims in place: a dimensional pun that slid between three unrelated twos, and a circular reading in which the cross-ratio of Ohm's and Joule's laws was reported as explaining electromagnetism's comfort on the floor. The second was **severed rather than quarantined**, on the ground that quarantine preserves a claim's standing and a circular claim has none to preserve.

**`L4-Riemann-3` — The Neighbours** honours a century of work reaching for the set-aside structure, and its load-bearing content is easy to miss. The Geometric Trinity's standard lesson is that curvature is a choice; its *second* lesson is the one carried forward — dynamical equivalence is not structural equivalence, and the difference is exactly a boundary term, so the Trinity is **one bulk and three different ledgers**, and a framework whose central claim is that the committed is the ledger cannot treat a difference of ledger as a difference of nothing. The part then shows how a dead proposal leaves its word behind: Weyl's *Eichinvarianz* was literally ruler invariance, Einstein's spectral-line objection killed the physics, London re-read the scale factor as a phase in 1927, and the field has carried *gauge* — the receipt for a deletion — for a hundred years without reading it. A correction of record follows, because the error is common: 

**The inertness of torsion is a property of Einstein–Cartan's particular action, not of torsion**, and anyone reaching for "torsion is non-dynamical" as a reason not to look **has quoted one Lagrangian and called it a law**. Each neighbour then restores a different piece — Poincaré gauge theory the generator, twistors chirality as a primitive, non-commutative geometry the commutator, the discrete programmes the hinge, the arrow and the order — with twistor theory named the nearest external relative, and causal set theory distinguished rather than ranked: it posits the partial order, the corpus posits the thing that writes it.

**Riemann and this work**; Both hosts count the same twenty-four, and the crossing has been re-derived file by file instead of admitted once. Contortion in four dimensions carries twenty-four components — one free index over an antisymmetric pair, $4 \times 6$, the six being $\binom{4}{2}$ — and the engine counts twenty-four too, but counts it as an object rather than a quantity: an outward square is one cube face read from one standing corner, six faces by four corners, and the rotation group acts on those twenty-four flags freely and therefore simply transitively, spending its whole order exactly once (`ENGINE-REFERENCE-AC` §2.3; expect there: the twenty-four outward squares, the free action, $|S_4|$ on the four body diagonals, and $A_4$ as the twelve manifest gates against the odd coset's twelve dual readings). A simply transitive action leaves no canonical base point, so a twenty-four carrying one is a torsor and not a parameter space, and collapsing it to a point is a **pinning** rather than a discard of dials — which the engine does openly, fixing the reference edge $P \to U$ and tagging the selector that rides it `[gauge]`, while the host does the same thing and files it as uniqueness.

The full account belongs outside this folder, because a friction report asserts no values and this is a statement about projection: it belongs at `L1-ConvergentDiscovery` Part III (expect there: the Projection Problem — every two-dimensional projection of the tetrahedron losing the volume between the faces while the projector's own face survives clearest, the projector's choice of projection never neutral, Wilber's hierarchy diagnosed as dimensional loss plus positional bias, and the Medicine Wheels reconciled by whether a culture reads the circle from inside or from outside). That last is the twelve-and-twelve the engine formalizes as its manifest and dual cosets, and the Fundamental Theorem is the projection taken from the metric face: it reads the connection off the metric and calls the rest derived, which is why the twenty-four arrives looking like nothing rather than like the volume between the faces.

## The Arithmetic Companion

**`L2-K4-Inferences`** runs the same floor-and-solid, route-and-commit audit on $\xi(s)$ without importing physics into number theory, and it is a cross-link this index proposes rather than a claim the geometric series already makes — the parent does not cite it.

It owns a seven-step inference procedure and its worked specimen. The four moves on $\xi$ close into a Klein four-group; sorting the plane by what each move fixes gives one bulk, two axes and a centre, with one subgroup class missing until the lift locates it; every orbit balances on $s = \tfrac12$ whatever the hypothesis turns out to be. The lift is where the work is: requiring each move to act as a rotation forces a third coordinate odd under conjugation, odd under the line-reflection and even under the functional equation, and the lowest monomial that qualifies is $z = xy$ — half the imaginary part of $(s - \tfrac12)^2$, so no new data enters. Two candidate lifts return zero volume and are reported as results. Volume then vanishes on exactly two axes, which is weaker than the hypothesis, and the second axis is cleared for $\xi$ by a theorem and left open for real characters, where a Landau–Siegel zero would sit. Part Four walks the cycle that writes the primes — unfold, hold, collapse, reseed, write — computing the explicit formula at $\psi_1(30.5)$ and closing 98.9% of the gap with a hundred dipoles, and ends by stating what the hypothesis is once the nouns are set down: **the claim that the cycle which writes the primes runs without gain or loss.**

## What Reading This Back Should Leave

Two purchases, sixty-eight years apart. One signed and priced in Riemann's own hand, about the line element. One unsignable at the time, because until 1922 there was no connection that could have carried torsion and therefore nothing available to decline. The field kept the geometry and lost the note.

What the second purchase removes is a kind of object rather than a dimension: a metric is a symmetric two-slot form and has no three-slot antisymmetric part, which is why the connection's antisymmetric piece is independent of it rather than derived from it. $T = 0$ keeps the turn and deletes the slide, so the geometry rotates and returns and records nothing. That was not a measurement. It was an account left unopened and reported as empty.

The arithmetic track finds the same shape in another host. A centre that is settled, proved and in daily use; a bulk whose occupancy is decided by a square nobody has paid at; and a century of equivalences moving the claim around the untaxed tier while the gate stays shut.

Neither track asks for belief, and neither disputes a confirmed number. What they add is a cost column — and a cost column is a bookkeeping device, not a discovery. What an action cannot do is commit, which is why the geometric case ends on a balance in Sardinia, and the arithmetic case ends holding a question open rather than closing it.
