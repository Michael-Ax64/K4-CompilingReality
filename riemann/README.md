## The Geometric and Arithmetic Audits

The architecture at the root of this work is Ohm's law seated on a fixed directed tetrahedron. The twelve relations among four poles in mutual determination are classical to the last term, and in logarithms they reduce to a plane of rank two. Seated on the solid, those same relations survive no relabeling at all: the automorphism group of the directed seating is trivial. The algebra is classical. The rigidity is not, and the rigidity is where everything downstream is decided (`ARCHITECTURE` §I).

$K_4$ is treated here as that abstract arrangement — four poles, a cyclic constraint net, a cost per commit — and the exercise is to inflate it into a host domain and find where it binds. In `L4-Riemann` and its six parts the host is differential geometry and gravitation. In `L2-K4-Inferences` the host is analytic number theory. In the Realization the host is the Riemann Hypothesis itself. **The binding is the deliverable.**

This folder replaces no theory. It does not claim the Riemann Hypothesis holds or fails, and it does not propose to replace General Relativity; $K_4$ is inflated into a host to find where the host made its purchase, and that reading is the yield. There is one exception, and it is the part a classical reader can check with their own tools: Part Five writes an action, derives what follows from it in closed form, and lands on one dimensionless dial with an existing experimental bound on it.

The two tracks meet in one image. `L4-Riemann-1` prices geometry's torsion-free decree: a screw with zero pitch is a circle, a rotation that returns and records nothing. The Realization (§XVI) finds the same decree on the zeros: seen from the hub at $s = \tfrac12$, a zero's slope is its rotor's pitch, and RH reads as zero pitch on every zero.

## The Purchase, Before Anything It Cost

Standard gravitation sets the connection symmetric, and that buys a great deal. Metric compatibility with a symmetric connection yields the unique connection built from the metric and its first derivatives; it can be made to vanish at a point in a suitable frame, which is the local form of the equivalence principle; the field equations stay second-order; no extra unconstrained tensor field has to be carried, propagated or bounded; and the resulting theory is confirmed to the precision the post-Newtonian tests reach. Those are good reasons, and this series takes them as the host's own.

The arithmetic track has the same shape. Centring on $\sigma = \tfrac12$ buys clean averaged prime laws, a real and lossless wave on the critical line, a positive proportion of zeros known to lie there, and the Bombieri–Vinogradov strength on average that much of the subject already runs on (`L2-K4-Inferences` §III.5). **No confirmed number is disputed anywhere in this folder.**

What the folder tracks is the other column of the receipt: what becomes invisible once a working formalism closes without a register to record it. A friction report has three parts — the host's rule, the point where the arrangement binds, and *what the host bought* — and the third part is the one that makes a cost worth itemizing. A friction report with an empty third part is not a friction report, and the purchase has to be stated in full before its cost can be read.

## Two Purchases, Sixty-Eight Years Apart

The tools are old — Cartan's torsion is 1922, Weyl's non-metricity and Finsler's direction-dependent length 1918 — so an argument assembled from such parts could have been assembled long ago. The reason it was not has a clean answer that accuses no one, and it is the honest shape of the history.

There are two decisions, and only one of them could have been signed. Riemann's is dated and in his own hand: in 1854 he set the direction-dependent line element aside in writing and priced the loss at "little new light." The closing of the small parallelogram is not his — there is no connection in that lecture. Christoffel supplied the symbols in 1869, Levi-Civita parallel transport in 1917, and both were building the only connection then available. The affine connection as a general object that *may* carry torsion is Cartan's, 1922: sixty-eight years after the line element, and seven years after General Relativity had been written in the symmetric connection and begun to be confirmed (`L4-Riemann-3` §V).

So the second decision was made before its alternative existed as an object. Riemann flagged what he could see, and there was nothing else on the table to flag. An unflagged assumption collects problems, each filed in its own subfield, rather than counter-evidence. Teitelboim's 1973 result — that the constraint algebra closes only with structure functions — is fifty years old and almost universally read as an obstacle to quantization rather than as a report on a classical assumption.

## Three Working Distinctions

Three distinctions carry the rest of the folder. Each is a boundary, and each is checkable.

**Nil is not zero.** A zero is an entry whose amount is zero, on an account that was opened. A nil is an account never opened. $T = 0$ is a nil presented as a zero, and the vacuum catastrophe is the same confusion run the other way (`L4-Riemann-1` §VII). A bound on a field returns a zero and cannot speak to a nil, so "nothing has been built" never licenses "the claim is false" — the first leaves a claim unregistered. The folder holds itself to this: unconstructed debts stay out of the kill list, and the unbuilt positivity container over $\mathbb{Z}$ is held explicitly nil.

**The floor is not the solid.** Written in logarithms, the twelve relations are four lines through one point, one per face, spanning a plane. They reduce to two, and that is the floor. The solid is built over it by the seats — the directed reading of every edge, and the single handedness bit the boundary's closure leaves free — and a rank count does not contain a seat (`L4-Riemann-2`). Counting the floor's rank as the solid's content is the deepest error in the case history.

**Symmetry is routing; the deciding square is a commit.** The classical statement of the Riemann Hypothesis, $\mathrm{Re}(s) = \tfrac12$, is a valid flat face reading measured from the corner $s = 0$. What the arithmetic track says is sharper: symmetry moves value without paying — free, unit slope — while a positivity commit is taxed, slope two, and only a commit can decide bulk occupancy. The equivalence mill — Li's criterion, Weil's form, Robin's inequality, Nyman–Beurling, de Branges — moves the claim across untaxed routes; two controls measure that structurally, the Davenport–Heilbronn function with the group and no Euler product and $\zeta(s-\tfrac14)\zeta(s+\tfrac14)$ with the group *and* an Euler product, both with zeros in the bulk (§7.1). **Nothing routable decides occupancy.** A real proof is the framework's arbiter and never a threat to it: the track predicts that a proof turns on a positivity and not on a new symmetry, and lists the reverse as a kill. And even after a genuine proof lands, simplicity is a separate conjecture: a zero of even multiplicity sits on the line, satisfies the hypothesis, and changes no sign (§IV.4). The Realization (§X) joins the two on the shaft: RH with simplicity holds exactly when every height carries one quantum.

## The Five Registers

Every claim in the geometric series belongs to one of five registers. The first four are `L4-K4-Infinities`' Four-Category Datasheet; the fifth is this series' own, and most of Part Four is written in it.

**Grammar** is counting and algebraic closure — four poles, six edges, twelve equations, index algebra, group characters, ranks. Native to any universe running the algebra, zero empirical input, anyone can re-run it. **Substrate** is the physical invariants, $c$, $\hbar$, $G$, $\kappa$, $Z_0$, $R_K$: consumed, never derived. **State** is the readout of a particular frame or experiment — a measured bound, a CHSH value, $H_0$ — and a claim resting on one is epoch-dependent. **Bridge** is dimensionless output computed from the first three with zero free parameters, and it is the only kind of number the series may claim as its own. **Friction** is the host's rule, the binding point, and what the host bought; it asserts no values and predicts nothing, and it is killed by showing the rule costs nothing or that the binding is an artefact of the inflation.

Only Part Five leaves Friction, to write a checkable action. The Realization files its claims in the corpus's claim registers (`llm-writing.md`, Claims Discipline) and closes on a register table of its own (§XVII).

## The Documents

**`L4-Riemann`** is the parent, and it holds the posture, the registers and the rules. It states the host's purchase first, separates the two trades sixty-eight years apart, and keeps three kinds of thing apart that are usually run together: **checks** are arithmetic anyone can re-run, **kills** are events in the world that would break a claim, and **unconstructed items** are debts. The sharpest kill in the file is the coupling's own zero — $F = \theta_F\theta_C\theta_M$ vanishes whenever any one bimedian is unsheared, so exhibiting geometric torsion sourced by a configuration held with one offset at zero ends the action.

**`L4-Riemann-1` — The Purchase** separates Riemann's signed 1854 trade from the unsigned closing of the parallelogram, and prices the second in three lines. Second covariant derivatives of scalars commute if and only if torsion vanishes, and on anything carrying an index the commutator survives as curvature. So $T = 0$ deletes the translational failure to close and keeps the rotational one: it sets the pitch to zero and leaves the turn, and **a screw with zero pitch is a circle** — it turns, it returns, and it records nothing. The part establishes nil against zero, and reads the engine's own reference pinning as a connection, finding it flat.

**`L4-Riemann-2` — The Floor and the Solid** is the bookkeeping part, placed early because later disagreements turn on it. It locates where the AC set breaks the floor: in polar coordinates the AC core *doubles* the floor rather than bending it, and the whole bend comes from the rectangular split and the additive layer, carried by one non-linear function, the power factor. It keeps the distinction between tensor type and manifold dimension precise — a symmetric two-slot form has no three-slot antisymmetric part, so the connection's antisymmetric piece is independent of the metric — and it shows that the totally antisymmetric torsion is a three-form, absent on any surface in a world of any dimension.

**`L4-Riemann-3` — The Neighbours** honours a century of work reaching for the set-aside structure. The Geometric Trinity's standard lesson is that curvature is a choice; its second lesson is the one carried forward — dynamical equivalence is not structural equivalence, and the difference is exactly a boundary term, so the Trinity is **one bulk and three different ledgers**. Weyl's *Eichinvarianz* was literally ruler invariance; Einstein's spectral-line objection killed the physics; London re-read the scale factor as a phase in 1927; and the field has carried *gauge* — the receipt for a deletion — for a hundred years. The inertness of torsion is a property of Einstein–Cartan's particular action, not of torsion. Each neighbour restores a different piece: Poincaré gauge theory the generator, twistors chirality as a primitive, non-commutative geometry the commutator, the discrete programmes the hinge, the arrow and the order.

**`L4-Riemann-4` — Frictions** is the register in action, four times. Reverse every arrow and the arrangement's bill changes on four of six edges, every COMMIT becoming a ROUTE, while the host's even-derivative equations cannot see it — the host bought time-reversal symmetry by having no cost column. The arrangement carries two clocks, a rate and a record; the host has one coordinate, and the problem of time is the receipt. The Fundamental Theorem projects a twenty-four-parameter family onto a point and prices the discard nowhere. And the host slices spacetime freely, assuming its constraint net orders, while its own constraint algebra has reported since 1973 that it does not.

**`L4-Riemann-5` — The Action** is the one part that leaves Friction. It couples a function of the three bimedian phase offsets to the Nieh–Yan density, finds the axial channel forced because it is the only one a scalar can use without a ghost, induces a kinetic term from geometry with no new scale, and recovers the torsion-free connection as **what geometry does when the offsets stop changing**. The coupling itself is forced: the Nieh–Yan density is odd under parity and under time reversal, central inversion exchanges the two edges of every bimedian, and $F = \theta_F\theta_C\theta_M$ is the unique carrier-symmetric survivor. Parity-symmetric populations average the coupling away and never average the cost away, so spin-polarized tests bound the dial at about $10^{-31}$ GeV.

**`L4-Riemann-6` — The Case** takes the argument in the order a reader new to the construction would. Four technical obstacles at full strength; the four objections that survived every audit, each read back to the host as a measurement of what the host assumes; the unfreeze, stated compactly with its cost itemized; and the framework's three crossings to the real axis, ending on a balance at Sos Enattos that belongs to no one.

## What Reading This Back Should Leave

Two purchases, sixty-eight years apart. One signed and priced in Riemann's own hand, about the line element. One unsignable at the time, because until 1922 there was no connection that could have carried torsion and therefore nothing available to decline. The field kept the geometry and lost the note.

What the second purchase removes is a kind of object rather than a dimension: a metric is a symmetric two-slot form and has no three-slot antisymmetric part, which is why the connection's antisymmetric piece is independent of it rather than derived from it. $T = 0$ keeps the turn and deletes the slide, so the geometry rotates and returns and records nothing. That was not a measurement. It was an account left unopened and reported as empty.

The arithmetic track finds the same shape in another host: a centre that is settled, proved and in daily use; a bulk whose occupancy is decided by a square nobody has paid at; and a century of equivalences moving the claim around the untaxed tier while the gate stays shut. The Realization stands at that centre and finds the decree again: the zeros rotate and return when their pitch is zero, and the shaft counts one quantum per height.

Neither track asks for belief, and neither disputes a confirmed number. What they add is a cost column — and a cost column that turns a 170-year-old definition into a priced decision, puts five problems from five literatures under one bill, and forces a coupling with no free dial is not mere bookkeeping. The bookkeeping is the discovery. What an action cannot do is commit, which is why the geometric case ends on a balance at Sos Enattos and the arithmetic case ends holding a question open rather than closing it.
