## XI. Finished Law, Never-Finishing Extension

`L4-K4-Infinities-1.md` §IV separates the completed infinity from the potential one, *"never finished, available at every step and total at none,"* and declines enumeration as a method. **The primes are both (FORCED).** As a law the set of primes is static and final: membership is decidable, every prime already is one, and the set is the unique fixed point of its own definition. As an extension it never completes: `sum(primes[1..x])` runs $P_N \to R_{N+1}$, each step built from the steps before it. The run compounds through the `COMMIT` square at three layers:
* the sieve fixes the primes up to $x^2$ from the primes up to $x$;
