## 1. The claim, in one breath

The Riemann Hypothesis is usually carried as a statement about the location of points: every nontrivial zero of ζ has real part ½. Held that way it is a fact about a scatter of dots in a strip, and every school that works on it works on where the dots are allowed to sit.

K₄ reads it as a statement about a **process**, and the shift is the whole model. The primes are not a scatter of points; they are the **frequencies** of a multiplicative texture that the field ζ expresses. RH is then the claim that **the cycle which writes the primes runs with zero gain and zero loss** — that the texture compiles to the integers without bias — and the critical line σ = ½ is the one locus where that losslessness is poised.

The sections below run the mechanism: the register it lives in (multiplicative), the crossing it turns on (the imaginary going real), the figure it unfolds into (one zero as a tetrahedron), and the single thing that can decide it (a commit) against the single thing that cannot (a symmetry).

## 2. Two views of one object

ζ(s) carries two readings of itself, and the completion factor is the exchange rate between them.

**The content view** is uncompleted ζ(σ+it): it keeps the active pole at s = 1, the Euler product over primes, and the secular rotor χ(½+it) = e^{−2iϑ(t)}. Along the critical line it winds as a helix, ζ(½+it) = e^{−iϑ(t)}Z(t), with a real amplitude Z(t) turning at rate ϑ′(t) ≈ ½ ln(t/2π). This is the active, phase-conductive side — the field doing work.

**The hold view** is the completed ξ(s) = ½ s(s−1) π^{−s/2} Γ(s/2) ζ(s): entire, pole-free, symmetric under s ↔ 1−s, and real on the line (Ξ(t) = ξ(½+it) is real and even). Completing to ξ strips the rotor, swallows the pole, and turns the helix into a standing, balanced shadow. The completion factor is the **compression operator** — it takes the content view's unfolded, active helix and compresses it to the hold view's balanced point.

Hold and content are not two objects. They are one substrate read from either side of its own crossing. The model lives on the content side, because that is where the writing happens; the hold side is the compiled, time-even slice.

## 5. The figure: one zero unfolds

## 6. Route and commit

The symmetry is free; the decision is taxed. In the engine a ROUTE moves value at unit slope and pays nothing; a COMMIT squares its operands at slope two and pays the Landauer bit. The whole symmetry apparatus of §5 — the group, the lift, the convergence, the balanced centroid — is route. It is forced, dimensionless, and carries no decision.

That symmetry cannot decide occupancy is a measurement, not an opinion. Two functions share ζ's exact group, lift, and centroid and still fill the bulk with zeros: the Davenport–Heilbronn function (the group, no Euler product) and ζ(s−¼)ζ(s+¼) (the group and an Euler product). Same route structure, occupied interiors. So neither the symmetry nor the bare presence of a product over primes empties the bulk.

What decides occupancy is a positivity — a commit, the mod-square of §4. On the line each zero pairs with itself and contributes a value times its own conjugate, non-negative; off the line it pairs with a different vertex of its orbit and the contribution is a cross-term that can go negative. **RH turns on a commit, and a routing argument cannot pay a commit.** This is the model's sharpest structural prediction: a proof, when it comes, turns on a positivity and not on a new symmetry.
