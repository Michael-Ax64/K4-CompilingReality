# Co-Inhabiting the Volume: How to Contribute

This repository is not a finished monument; it is a running engine. The initial thermodynamic labor of clearing terminological debris and building the floor has been paid. The invitation now is to co-inhabit the volume.

Because this framework models the Algebra of Four-Fold Distinction, contribution to it must also be four-fold. We do not accept arbitrary edits to the core text, but we actively invite structural contributions across all four faces of the topology.

When you arrive, you must know which portal you are entering through.

## The Four Portals of Contribution

### 1. The U-Portal (Air / Logos / Structure)
**Who:** Software Engineers, Mathematicians, Logicians, Systems Architects.
**The Work:** You build the syntax and the tools.
* Expand the `/code/` and `/manifolds/` directories.
* Build practical tools for your own use. Integrate APIs; analyze feeds, posts, and documents. Share the implementations.
* Run blinded permutation sweeps (`ProofS`) on novel, untested 4-element systems.
* Formalize the projection operators linking the algebra to physical tensors.
**The Rule:** Your code must compile. Your math must close.

### 2. The I-Portal (Water / Pathos / Relational Flow)
**Who:** Clinicians, Community Builders, Therapists, Translators, Writers.
**The Work:** You provide the therapeutic bridge and subjective translation. The math remains abstract until grounded in lived experience.
* Engage the framework's core concepts. Share your notes and document your learning process.
* Write new `*Subjective` registers and `TooTrue` mythopoetic diagnostics for specific industries or pathologies.
* Apply the 12-facet diagnostic to active human systems (individuals, organizations, relationships) to resolve plane-lock.
* Translate the framework's structures out of Indo-European linguistic packaging to verify universality (`ProofS` Step 5).
**The Rule:** Do not sacrifice rigor for poetic comfort. Subjective, felt, or intuitive registers are welcomed, provided they anchor cleanly in the codex.

### 3. The P-Portal (Fire / Kairos / Initiation)
**Who:** Visionaries, Founders, Disruptors, Domain Experts.
**The Work:** You supply raw, ungrounded anomalies—the problems your field cannot solve because it is trapped in a Kessler Cascade.
* Bring heavily plane-locked domains and test them against the 12 equations.
* Force the framework into unmapped territory.
* Drive the generator to produce new structural hypotheses.
**The Rule:** You must allow the framework to dismantle the favored nouns and "hidden variables" of your domain.

### 4. The R-Portal (Earth / Ethos / Ground)
**Who:** Funders, Infrastructure Providers, Resource Allocators.
**The Work:** You pay the Landauer Tax.
In a volumetric system, financial and material resources are stored metabolic energy—thermodynamic fuel.
* Fund rent, compute, and hardware infrastructure.
* Support the continued development of the `AlgebraicSwarmController`.
* Fund Step 5 of `ProofS` by hiring human domain-natives across distant linguistic lineages.
None of this actualizes purely in the abstract space of Air ($U$). It requires the material bedrock of Earth ($R$). Bringing resources provides the ground for the system to actualize.
**The Rule:** You fund the infrastructure; you do not buy the right to alter the algebra.

**Patches to the foundational algebra (L1–L3) are not accepted.**

Locate the domain, trace the breakdown, and present the anomaly. A framework that invites rigorous engagement owes its users an explicit kill condition. This is ours.

Look before you rule. Run the generator. Step inside.
