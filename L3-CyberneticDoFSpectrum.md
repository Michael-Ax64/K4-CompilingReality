### 1. 0 DoF — The Stone / Crystal Limit
$Q = 0$. Zero internal decoupled buffer. Zero delay between stimulus and response. A stone, a thermostat, an automated reflex loop, or a fully committed crystal (`ProofC_Crystal`). All potential has been collapsed into $P$-ledger mass. The system has state; it has no capacity to hold alternatives.

### 2. 2 DoF — The DC Algebraic Network & K₃ Markov Blanket
Four variables $(P, I, U, R)$ governed by two independent constraints ($U = IR$ and $P = UI$). Specifying any two variables forces the remaining two. Topologically, this is the 2D $K_3$ Markov Blanket surface: two independent boundary DoF (sensory and active states).

### 3. 3 DoF — The Δ³ Polyhedral Volume / Single-Edge Quasi-Clone
Holding four states in general position without flattening requires a 3-simplex ($\Delta^3$) in $\mathbb{R}^3$: 3 spatial DoF for the vertex coordinates. A single $K_4$ edge has four states ($\emptyset, \to, \leftarrow, \leftrightarrow$; `L3-EdgeStateSpace`); the absent state is orthogonal to the three presence states, and holding all four requires a 3D volume.

### 4. 6 DoF — The Complex hQ Vector Buffer
$\mathbf{Q} = Q_i \mathbf{i} + Q_j \mathbf{j} + Q_k \mathbf{k}$ carries three complex components. Each complex component has a real and an imaginary part:

$$3 \text{ complex components} \times 2 = 6 \text{ real DoF}$$

This is the 6-bit Boolean hypercube ($2^6 = 64$), and the address the corpus points at when reading string theory's "6 extra compactified dimensions": the 6 real DoF of the $h\mathbf{Q}$ phase-space buffer (`Q3-ScalarSector`, `Q10-PlanckScale`).

### 5. 12 DoF — The K₄ Edge Space / Twelve Real Parameters
$K_4$ has 6 undirected edges. Each undirected edge carries one complex impedance $Z = R + jX$, or equivalently one real weight and one chirality bias — the same twelve, two ways of naming them:

$$6 \text{ undirected edges} \times (R + jX) = 6 \text{ complex} = 12 \text{ real parameters}$$

Assigning a 2-bit state-space to each of the 6 edges gives $6 \times 2 = 12$ bits for the discrete backend ($2^{12} = 4096$ Many-Worlds states; `L2-BitCombos`, `yijing/The_Line_Is_A_Directed_Edge`). The continuous and discrete counts agree at 12.

### 6. ∞ DoF — The Plenum / The Indeterminate
$H_{\text{incoh}}$ is the uncompiled, scale-invariant phase-space buffer of the universe (`L4-ThePlenum`). It holds every physically valid state in live, reversible interference. No finite count bounds it. The ladder terminates here because the last rung is not a rung but the medium the whole structure sits in.

### The 2 Global Anchors

Two independent Degrees of Freedom fall out of the DC network: $4 \text{ variables} - 2 \text{ constraints} = 2$ (`L3-FormalFoundations` §I). Specifying two variables forces the other two. In the parameter decomposition, these two DoF appear as the two global anchors:

2. **Global Systemic Chirality ($\mathcal{T}_0$).** The baseline handedness of the entire system — the reference chirality against which all other biases are measured.

Fixing these two anchors sets the system's overall size and overall handedness.

### The 5 + 5 Internal Split

Once the two global anchors are fixed, ten internal edge parameters remain. They split into two groups of five.

**5 Relative Edge Weights.** The relative "thickness" or "impedance" of the remaining five undirected edges, measured against the reference edge $W_0$:

$$\left[ \frac{w_2}{W_0}, \ \frac{w_3}{W_0}, \ \frac{w_4}{W_0}, \ \frac{w_5}{W_0}, \ \frac{w_6}{W_0} \right]$$

**5 Relative Chirality Biases.** The relative directional asymmetry of the remaining five edges, measured against the reference bias $\mathcal{T}_0$:

$$\left[ \frac{\tau_2}{\mathcal{T}_0}, \ \frac{\tau_3}{\mathcal{T}_0}, \ \frac{\tau_4}{\mathcal{T}_0}, \ \frac{\tau_5}{\mathcal{T}_0}, \ \frac{\tau_6}{\mathcal{T}_0} \right]$$

Under the AC reading, these five relative chirality biases *are* the five relative reactances $X_e / X_0$ — same parameters, one bookkeeping.

### Where Chirality Comes From

Real physical, biological, and cognitive edges are directionally asymmetric. Chirality bias $\tau_e$ measures the normalized cost difference between forward and reverse traversal:

$$\tau_e = \frac{e^+ - e^-}{e^+ + e^-} \in [-1, +1]$$

* $\tau_e = 0$: symmetric edge ($e^+ = e^-$).
* $\tau_e = +1$: one-way street forward ($e^- \to \infty$).
* $\tau_e = -1$: one-way street reverse ($e^+ \to \infty$).

Under the AC reading (`L4-Dimensionality` §Imaginary Plane), $\tau_e$ *is* the reactance $X_e$. Traversed one way the edge reads inductive ($+jX_L$); the other way capacitive ($-jX_C$). Same $(R, X)$ pair, two directional views. The count stays at 12.

## IV. A Consistency Check, Not a Loop Closure

| Address | Claim | Instrument | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `L4-ScalingInvariants.md` §A1 | The two log-space midpoint constraints $u = (p+r)/2$ and $i = (p-r)/2$ sum to $u + i = p$, exponentiating back to $P = UI$. | The DC Closure | CONFIRMED as consistency check on the log form of $P = UI$. |

Take logs of $U = \sqrt{PR}$ and $I = \sqrt{P/R}$:

$$u = \tfrac{1}{2}(p + r), \qquad i = \tfrac{1}{2}(p - r)$$

Sum:

$$u + i = \tfrac{1}{2}(p + r) + \tfrac{1}{2}(p - r) = \tfrac{2p}{2} = p$$

Exponentiate:

$$e^p = e^{u+i} = e^u \cdot e^i \quad \Longrightarrow \quad P = U \times I$$

**What this is.** A verification that the two root-transformers ($U = \sqrt{PR}$ and $I = \sqrt{P/R}$) are algebraically consistent with the source equation $P = UI$ they were derived from. The check runs in log-space and confirms that the 45° basis rotation from $(p, r)$ to $(u, i)$ preserves the fundamental relation.

**What this is not.** A "self-closing topological loop" and not a landed result. The identity $u + i = p$ is *the log form of $P = UI$*, not a new theorem. Adding the two derived constraints and getting back the equation they were derived from is what "derived" means; it would be alarming if it did not hold.

The $r$-cancellation (the pivot around which $u$ and $i$ rotate) is real geometry, and A1 already names it: *"Assert and Yield are conjugate bases of the same plane, related by a 45° rotation."* Keep the geometry, keep the check, do not promote either to a discovery.
