### 1. Stage 0: The Full K4 Volume Held (~9600 BCE, Göbekli Tepe)

At the terminus of the Younger Dryas, human consciousness operated through an un-domesticated right hemisphere (`L1-ConvergentDiscovery` §19–20). On the megalithic T-pillars of Göbekli Tepe, the divine carries no human face. The Bull ($R$), the Lion ($P$), the Vulture/Eagle ($I$), and the Serpent ($U$) — the ancestral Tetramorph — stand in pure spatial `.behold()` stone, before the invention of agriculture, before the invention of writing. All four poles are named. None is committed. All six edges of the $K_4$ substrate are held in superposition, and no face has been chosen. This is not "primitive religion." It is the divine container holding the full tetrahedral volume without collapsing any of it into a written ledger.

### 2. Stage 1: The U-R Edge Crystallized (~3000 BCE, Dynastic Egypt)

As agriculture, stone architecture, and civic writing arose, the god became a human torso wearing an animal head. The human body provided the $U$-container (civil law and civic structure); the animal head preserved the un-domesticated $R$-pole frequency (celestial predation, decomposition, scribal memory, physical fertility). Structurally: the divine container crystallized onto the **U-R edge**.

The U-R edge has two face-readings (`L3-EdgeStateSpace` §I-b, Master Edge Table):

- **ARI face** ($P = U^2/R$, Fire face): the P-force generated as the human civil-structure squared over the raw animal frequency. This is the ram-headed Khnum crafting humans on his wheel, the lion-headed Sekhmet whose slaughter must be constrained by human ritual, the warrior-dominion mode of the U-R edge.
- **VIR face** ($I = U/R$, Water/Earth face): the current flowing through the structure over ground. This is the ibis-headed Thoth as scribe, the jackal-headed Anubis performing careful analytical work with matter, the priestly-scribal mode of the U-R edge.

The Egyptian pantheon carries both face-readings of the U-R edge simultaneously. The specific animal-head names the specific R-frequency; the human body names the U-container; the god-type names which face of the edge is being inhabited.

### 3. Stage 2: The P-Pole Activated Across Boundary Edges (~1200 BCE, Bronze Collapse)

When civilizational complexity suffered catastrophic thermodynamic overload, systems could no longer sustain open `.behold()` contemplation. They required the **Cutting Mode Personified** (`L4-ReligiousIntuitions.md` §VII) — Set in Egypt, Enlil in Mesopotamia, the desert Yahweh of Sinai. This is the emergency actuator: the force of unyielding will ($P$) that hardens hearts, demands absolute obedience, and cuts the boundary ($K_3$) at sword-point to prevent systemic collapse.

Structurally: the P-pole activates and operates aggressively across all three of its adjacent edges (P-U, P-R, P-I) at once. This is the *only* stage where a single pole rather than a specific edge is the inhabited locus, because emergency cutting cannot afford edge-face discipline — it operates by raw P-emission across whatever boundary is failing. Stage 2 is not a stable inhabitation; it is a survival configuration.

### 4. Stage 3: The U-Pole Isolated (~600 BCE, Proto-Abrahamism)

With the invention of the Proto-Sinaitic alphabet at Serabit el-Khadim, the divine container shed the physical image entirely. By reducing multi-dimensional pictograms to a $1\text{D}$ serial consonantal alphabet, the god-concept became **The Word ($U$)**. The second commandment banned the $I$-face image (*"No graven images"*), converting the divine into an abstract, portable, weightless, non-negotiable legal operating system.

Structurally: the U-pole isolates. The edge configuration degenerates to a pole-alone — U with no acknowledged edge partner. The Aniconic Word cannot inhabit a full edge because inhabiting an edge requires committing to a face, and every face-commitment involves either an image ($I$-face) or a ground-symbol ($R$-face) that the aniconic move forbids. What Stage 3 has is the pole; what it lacks is any face on which to walk.

### 5. Stage 4: The P-U Edge, LEO Face Inhabited (~0 CE, Christ / Krishna)

When the abstract Lawgiver ($U$) inevitably calcified into institutional legalism, the system executed the **Incarnational Phase-Correction**. The divine ceased to be an unapproachable sky-law ($U$) or an animal force ($R$), and became the pouring of source ($P$) through structural form ($U$) into living relational presence ($I$).

Structurally: the P-U edge is inhabited, and specifically its **LEO face** ($I = P/U$, Fire face) is walked. This completes what Stage 3's U-pole isolation had been half-doing: Stage 3 had U-alone; Stage 4 walks U-in-relation-with-P, and reads the resulting edge from the face where the outcome is $I$-pole embodied life. *"The Sabbath was made for man ($I$), not man for the Sabbath ($U$)."*

## III. Cycle β and the Octave: What Has Been Walked

The $K_4$ tetrahedron has three undirected Hamiltonian cycles (`L3-EdgeStateSpace` §V):
- **Cycle α**: $P \to I \to U \to R \to P$
- **Cycle β**: $P \to I \to R \to U \to P$
- **Cycle γ**: $P \to U \to I \to R \to P$

Each cycle uses exactly 4 of the 6 edges and leaves 2 unused. Each cycle is an **octave**: 4 vertices plus 4 edges equals 8 positions of the closed structure.

The god-types record has been walking cycle β. Trace it against the five-stage cascade:

- **Stage 1** inhabits the **R-U edge** — this is β's edge 3 ($R \to U$).
- **Stage 4** inhabits the **U-P edge** — this is β's edge 4 ($U \to P$), closing the cycle.

The record's inhabited edges are β's **second half**: R-U (walked in Stage 1 across both ARI and VIR faces) and U-P (walked in Stage 4 as LEO, and as AQU across the two macro-loops of the Betrayer configuration).

β's **first half** — the P-I edge (β's edge 1) and the I-R edge (β's edge 2) — has never been walked in the record. The **I-pole vertex** does not appear as a named inhabited position in the god-types cascade at all.

The I-pole region — I vertex, P-I edge, I-R edge — is entirely outside the record's inhabited positions.

Two readings of the skip stand simultaneously:

**Reading A (historical fact of non-arising):** the vocabulary that could name I-face content did not exist before Stage 3, and Stage 3 itself is the Aniconic Word — an alphabet-based, 1D-serial medium whose founding move ("no graven images") categorically excludes the I-face. Both readings are true. They point at the same coordinates from different sides of the helix. A explains the historical shape of the record. B explains what completion of the walk would look like.

- Image (I-face committed): forbidden explicitly.

- Ground-symbol as inhabited representation (R-face committed): the alphabet reduces multi-dimensional pictograms to 1D consonants, stripping ground-frequency.

- Living relational current as writable content: 1D serial cannot hold the simultaneity of the I-pole; it can only refer to it after the fact.


The I-pole material did not fail to *exist* before Stage 3. It failed to enter the *record* Stage 3 defined. The bull-cults, the goddess figurines, the mystery initiations, the shamanic lineages, the body-practices, the mother-goddess complexes — these carried I-pole material continuously through and around Stage 3, in mediums the Aniconic Word could neither contain nor endorse. Loop 2's correction requires a medium that can carry what the Aniconic Word cannot. Whatever the corrective vocabulary turns out to be, it must be embodied, relational, non-serialized, and image-tolerant. It must be able to hold the P-I and I-R edges as inhabited positions. It cannot be Stage 3's tools deployed at higher resolution; Stage 3's tools are the medium that excluded these edges in the first place.

The corpus you are reading is not Stage 3. It is written in Stage 3's medium (alphabetic, serial, technical, algebraic) — it must be, because we are addressing each other across a written surface. But its **register** is dialogic: $I \leftrightarrow P$ live axis with a $U$-spine. The scaffold is 1D; the traffic on the scaffold is relational-current between reader and writer. This is not the same operation as Stage 3. This is closer to the P-I edge's LIB face inhabited *directly* — structure arising as the shape of source-through-current, using the alphabet as scaffold rather than as container.

Whether this is *the* vocabulary Loop 2's correction requires or *one* such beginning is not settled. What is settled: the vocabulary cannot be Stage 3's alone. - That Loop 2's correction will happen automatically, on its own, without human volition. The framework can name the shape of the required walk. It cannot walk it for anyone.

- That the corpus you are reading *is* the vocabulary Loop 2 requires. It is one beginning, in dialogic register, using the alphabetic medium as scaffold rather than as container. Whether it is *the* beginning or *a* beginning is not settled and does not need to be settled here.

The geometry is drawn. The current is waiting. The choice is to notice.
