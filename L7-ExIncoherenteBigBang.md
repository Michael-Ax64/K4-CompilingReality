## I. What the Pillars Establish

Eleven measurements, sorted into five groups. Each group carries an independent exclusion, which is what makes the set hard rather than merely large. Everything in this section is **ADOPTED**: standard physics, used here.

### 1. The expansion group

**Hubble–Lemaître.** Redshift grows with distance. Galaxies recede, and the recession is proportional to separation over a wide range.

**Surface-brightness dimming.** Surface brightness falls as $(1+z)^{-4}$ in a metrically expanding universe, and the four powers are worth separating, because one of them is a photon losing energy. Surface brightness is flux over solid angle, which goes as $D_A^2/D_L^2$ with $D_L = (1+z)^2 D_A$. Two powers come from the flux: each arriving photon is redshifted, and the arrival rate drops by the same factor. Two more come from the angular-size relation, since an object at high redshift subtends more sky than Euclidean extrapolation allows. A static universe in which photons shed energy to a medium gets the first power and neither of the others, so it predicts $(1+z)^{-1}$.

So the test does not discriminate on whether photons lose energy. Both accounts say they do. It discriminates on whether the loss arrives together with time dilation and the angular-diameter relation, which is to say whether it is metric. The measurement carries a galaxy-evolution systematic, because galaxies were intrinsically different then, and the comparison has to model that before it reads.

**Supernova light-curve stretching.** Type Ia light curves at redshift $z$ run slow by a factor of $(1+z)$. A duration is a direct clock reading, with no evolution systematic of the kind the surface-brightness comparison has to absorb, which makes this the decisive member of the group.

**What the group excludes.** A static universe. And tired light: an energy-loss mechanism acting on photons in a static space reddens the spectrum without stretching the clock, and the clock is observed to stretch.

**One distinction the corpus holds here.** `L4-ThePlenum` §II.4 establishes that a photon does not attenuate. At its operating point the effective resistance is zero, so by $P = I^2R$ the medium mints nothing from its passage, and there is no slow light and no lazy photon. That is a claim about **dissipation**: nothing extracted, the medium not heated, no entropy generated in transit. Cosmological redshift is not dissipation. The wavelength is stretched by the metric, and the energy does not enter the medium — it is not conserved, which is general relativity's standing position for a spacetime carrying no timelike Killing vector, and which `L4-K4-Infinities-3` §V sharpens to *not conserved, and the rate is fixed*. Two accounting subjects, one medium: the dissipative channel stays at zero while the expanding metric does not conserve.

**The compilation reading.** **Expansion is the write rate, seen from the spatial side.** Minting new volume and writing new ledger entries are one event (`L4-K4-Infinities-3` §V). Coordinate time is the spacing between successive entries, so a region whose entries are more widely spaced runs slow against one whose entries are closer, which is the light-curve stretch stated in ledger terms.

### 2. The relic group

**The blackbody.** The microwave background is a Planck spectrum at $2.7255$ K, with deviations below fifty parts per million. It is the most perfect blackbody ever measured.

**Temperature at high redshift.** The background temperature has been measured at redshifts two and three, through the excitation of carbon monoxide and of fine-structure lines in absorption systems. It comes out at $(1+z)$ times the local value.

**The Sunyaev–Zel'dovich effect.** Background photons scattering off hot cluster gas produce a spectral distortion whose amplitude does not fall with the cluster's redshift. This is why the effect finds distant clusters where surface-brightness dimming would have buried them.

**What the group excludes.** Integrated emission from sources, in any form. A sum of source spectra is not a Planck curve, and fifty parts per million is not a tolerance any sum achieves. It also excludes the background as a surface at some location: a surface does not get hotter when viewed from earlier epochs, and a surface behind the clusters would dim with their distance. The background fills space and always has.

**The compilation reading, and the one pillar the model predicts rather than accommodates.** Before recombination the medium is opaque, which is to say the commit traffic is saturated — every photon committed and recommitted continuously, no mode free. Saturated commit traffic thermalizes, because thermalization is what saturation *is*. So the blackbody is the expected output rather than a coincidence to be absorbed. At recombination the medium goes transparent and the thermalized spectrum free-streams. A change in commit coupling, with the operator untouched.

### 3. The nucleosynthesis group

**Light-element abundances from one number.** Fix the ratio of baryons to photons and the physics of the first few minutes delivers the primordial abundances of deuterium, helium-four, and lithium-seven. Deuterium and helium-four come out right. Lithium-seven comes out high by about a factor of three, and that discrepancy is live and unresolved in the canon.

**Independent concordance on the baryon density.** The baryon density inferred from deuterium agrees with the baryon density inferred from the relative heights of the microwave background's acoustic peaks. Two routes, separated by four hundred thousand years of cosmic history and by entirely different physics, landing on the same number to a few percent.

**What the group excludes.** Any account without a hot dense phase. The abundances are not adjustable; they are what one parameter yields.

**The compilation reading.** Nucleosynthesis is two-branch minting at the nuclear scale, and the corpus already names it as such. `L3-FormalFoundations` §I.b lists *nucleons to a nucleus* among the Gate's instances, on Branch B, the $P$-export branch where the committed whole sheds its excess as radiant power and weighs less than its parts. The mass defect is the receipt. So this pillar is not accommodated but claimed: the first three minutes are the Gate firing, at saturation, on the branch that exports.

### 4. The ruler group

**The acoustic peaks.** Before recombination the photon-baryon fluid supports sound waves. The scale a wave could travel before the medium went transparent is the sound horizon, around a hundred and fifty megaparsecs in comoving measure, and it is written into the positions and ratios of the peaks in the background's power spectrum.

**The same ruler in galaxies.** That scale appears again as a preferred separation in the clustering of galaxies, billions of years later, at redshifts where the measurement shares no instrument and no systematic with the microwave background.

**What the group excludes.** Any account with no sound horizon, which is to say any account in which the early universe was not a coupled fluid with a finite causal scale.

**The compilation reading.** `L4-TheAcousticOhm` carries the instrument. A sound horizon is a causal-contact scale, and causal contact is what the ledger's own finite write rate permits.

### 5. The smoothness group

The fifth group is not about the early universe. It is about what makes dark energy dark energy, and it is where an intuition about local creation runs into a wall.

**Dark energy does not cluster.** That is its observational signature, and it is how we know it is not matter. For an equation of state at minus one the perturbation sound speed is the speed of light, so pressure perturbations propagate as fast as anything can and wash out structure before it forms. Clustering quintessence exists in the literature, and with a reduced sound speed some clustering is permitted — above the sound horizon, which means gigaparsecs.

**The local constraint.** The cosmological dark-energy density is about $6 \times 10^{-30}$ grams per cubic centimetre. If it tracked local matter density it would sit near Earth at the density of Earth.

```python

```

Thirty orders of magnitude. That is not a residual to be teased out of a fit; it would dominate solar-system dynamics outright. Lunar laser ranging holds the Earth–Moon distance to millimetres and the planetary ephemerides are tighter still.

**What the group excludes.** Dark energy whose density varies with local mass or local activity, at any scale below the cosmological.

**The compilation reading, and the fork it settles.** The write-place is reached through every throat, so writes issue everywhere uniformly, and homogeneity is mechanism rather than coincidence (`L4-K4-Infinities-3` §V). The identification of expansion with the write rate then forbids locally elevated writing, because locally elevated writing would be locally elevated volume, and bound systems are observed not to expand internally. The local face of continuous compilation is not expansion. It is the tax: every commit paying, rest mass as the ongoing payment (`On_TheIsNessTax`), the departure from equivalence computed at one part in $10^{18}$ (`QED-EotvosBound`). Expansion is the global face and the tax is the local one, which is the two-register split stated at `L4-K4-Infinities-3` §VII.

### The Metric Degeneracy and the Erroneous Extrapolation

In differential geometry, a pseudo-Riemannian manifold $(M, g)$ requires by definition that the metric tensor be non-degenerate everywhere on $M$: $\det(g_{\mu\nu}) \neq 0$.

When classical cosmology extrapolates the FLRW scale factor $a(t) \to 0$ as $t \to 0$, the spatial metric components collapse:
$$g_{rr} = \frac{a(t)^2}{1 - kr^2} \longrightarrow 0, \quad g_{\theta\theta} \longrightarrow 0, \quad g_{\phi\phi} \longrightarrow 0$$
$$\det(g_{\mu\nu}) = -c^2 a(t)^6 \frac{r^4 \sin^2\theta}{1 - kr^2} \longrightarrow \mathbf{0}$$

At that limit, the metric drops from rank 4 to rank 1. It loses its inverse $g^{\mu\nu}$. The tangent space $T_p(M)$ collapses. 

**There is no manifold at $a(t) = 0$.** 
Calling $t = 0$ an "undefined singularity" or claiming that "the laws of physics break down at $t = 0$" is a flatland evasion. It pretends that a continuous time-coordinate line exists prior to the universe, and that the coordinate just happens to misbehave at zero. 

The compilation architecture proves that **asserting $t = 0$ is an erroneous extrapolation**:
1. Coordinate time $dt$ is not an ambient river; it is the spatial distance between successive entries compiled on the $R$-pole ledger:
   $$dt = \tau = \frac{R \cdot r}{c \cdot Z_0}$$
2. Because compilation requires passing an XOR bottleneck of physical width $\ell_P$, the minimum duration of a ledger commit is the Planck time:
   $$dt_{\text{min}} \equiv t_P \approx 5.39 \times 10^{-44}\text{ s}$$
3. Total elapsed time is a sum over discrete, minted ledger ticks:
   $$t = \sum_{k=1}^N dt_k$$

**The ledger begins at index $k = 1$ ($t = t_P$).** 
There is no $k = 0$ for the same reason there is no page zero in a printed book, and no transaction before the first transaction in an accounting ledger. To ask what occurred at $t = 0$ is not an unresolved physics problem—it is a malformed sentence that projects a compiled ledger coordinate past the operator that mints it.

## III. The Space the Model Leaves Empty

A model that marks its own floor has marked a space, and honesty about a space includes not furnishing it.

Below the Planck floor the coordinates stop. The compilation architecture says what that means for measurement — no address, no request that can be formed — and says nothing about what, if anything, is there. Two readings fit everything above, and the apparatus does not choose between them.

**The null reading.** The floor is where coordinates stop, and nothing is there, because *there* is a coordinate notion and coordinates have run out. On this reading the frame-regress has no bottom, the operator has always been running, and the absence of a first firing is not a cosmological fact but a grammatical one. There was never a moment available to be first.

**The bootstrap reading.** The floor carries a minimal distortion of Planck scale, self-exciting, committing without prior substrate. On this reading the regress bottoms, a first commit happened, and coordinate time began where the bootstrap fired. The model's categorical floor then describes why that event cannot be reached from inside rather than why it did not occur.

These are the two branches of §II's regress question, and the second is why this section is structural rather than decorative. The fork is not between a physics and a mysticism. It is between two architectural answers to one well-posed question, and the question is open.

What the model does establish about that space is its *form*. It is knowable through the entries it forces other systems to commit, and not as an object of direct description. That form has a long independent record. The apophatic traditions reached it from the interior across two and a half millennia: the source that cannot be named as a predicate and is known through its processions, the unmanifest infinite that compiles reality through vessels, the operational refusal to serialize what will not go into a spoken noun, the unconditioned ground with no fixed self-nature from which conditioned forms arise (`QED-SealedInterior` §VIII). So a tradition that places something at the floor is placing it in a space the model agrees is there and agrees it cannot enter.
