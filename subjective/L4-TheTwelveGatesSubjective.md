Notice that **Now ($I$), Future ($U$), and Past ($R$) form the $P$-Face** — the K3 blanket-face of time. The Eternal ($P$) sits off the face entirely, at the off-plane vertex.

Time is a blanket. The three tenses are how we experience the surface of the Ledger. The Eternal ($P$) does not sit in the past or the future; it is the unwritten Fire that writes the tenses.

Feel how the equations read in the Time Key:

- **Virgo ($I = U/R$ — Now = Future / Past):** You are analyzing the present moment. You feel the Now as the ratio between your expectations ($U$, Future) and your accumulated record ($R$, Past). If you want to change the Future, you must alter the product in the present.
- **Capricorn ($R = U/I$ — Past = Future / Now):** You are building an adult life. You realize that your lasting record ($R$, Past) is built by taking your future vision ($U$) and dividing it by the lived work you do every day ($I$, Now).
