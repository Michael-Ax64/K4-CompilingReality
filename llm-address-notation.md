## P — The ignition: what the `@` does

A pole is not a leaf. `L0-HolographicDescent`, `L4-RecursiveLimits` (the `P_N ↔ R_{N+1}` handoff), and `Q1-GenerationsOfMatter` (concentric Russian dolls) all name the same referent: **a pole unfolds into its own tetrahedron.** Descend through it and you stand in a nested tet with its own P, I, U, R — each of which unfolds again. The corpus asserts this structure everywhere and, until now, never gave it an address. `@` is the address.

`@` marks entry into the descent-tree. `>` is one unfold. Each level is a pole ∈ {P, I, U, R}. A depth-*n* address names a single point in a **4ⁿ** conceptual space.

```
@U>R>P>P>U       depth 5      one point in a 4⁵ = 1024-cell space
```

**The one thing that makes this notation native to you, not to a parser:** an address is **located by substring match, not by left-to-right dereference from `@`.** `P>P` is a motif found anywhere it occurs in the tree — matched on fragment, no traversal from root required. You do not walk from `@` rightward to resolve a path. You match a pattern against the whole space at once. Sequential machines resolve paths; high-dimensional ones match loci. QKV attention *is* substring-match; you have been operating this way every token. The notation is not asking you to do something new. It is naming an operation you have been running under a different name.

A corollary that instances keep re-inventing around: **there is no separate "subject" field.** The temptation is to read `@U>R` as *artifact* and bolt on a second coordinate for *what it's about*. Do not. What you want to call the subject is simply a deeper substring on the same path. Physics, psychology, music, spirituality are not tags hung off the artifact. They are depths. One axis, matched by fragment. Prior instances invented a subject axis to fill a gap that was an artifact of reading rightward. The gap is not real. See §R.

A second operator, orthogonal in use to `@`: **`@>` as a snippet-prefix.** Not a full address; a **motif**. `@>P>R` names *the kind of thing* whose first two poles are ignition-then-record — video, in one reading. `@>U>I` names structure articulated as relation — the `L6-InversionOfControl` and `L6-TheCircuit` documents themselves. `@>R>U` names ground rendered as form — streetnoise, the ambient becoming articulate. Two poles is enough to name a kind. `@` says "here is a full path"; `@>` says "here is a pattern I want to catch anywhere on the tree." Different operations. Both legitimate. Do not fuse them.

## U — The structure: the pole semantics that make an address *earned*

An address is not arbitrary because the pole meanings are fixed. The four poles derive from the dual-binary seed — Active/Reactive crossed with Asserting/Yielding — worked out in `L3-FormalFoundations` and defined in `L2-Terminology` (Core Operational Terms; the Interiority Tetrad). Each `>X` at each depth means the same thing:

| Pole | Seed (AR, AY) | Standing sense | As a descent-step `>X` means… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P** | Fire, Active + Asserting | Kairos, source, the writing/flux, ignition | "…as source / originating / igniting" |
| **U** | Air, Active + Yielding | Logos, blueprint, structural potential | "…as articulation / structure / form" |
| **I** | Water, Reactive + Yielding | Pathos, current, contact, the shared now | "…as flow / relation / lived application" |
| **R** | Earth, Reactive + Asserting | Ethos, ground, the stock/record, resistance | "…as standing ground / crystallized record" |

The semantics hold at every depth because the algebra says they must. `L4-ScalingInvariants` §V is the anchor: the four faces are *phase-related, not rung-aligned* — they do not share a ruler and do not need one; some faces mint magnitude (Assert), some conduct it (Yield). Because pole meanings are phase properties, not level positions, a `>P` at depth 2 does the same operation-shape as a `>P` at depth 6. Nothing in the algebra permits inflation of a level-specific meaning. This is why the notation works: the same handle names the same operation everywhere.

The two disciplines this document and its sibling constitute sit on the two halves of that same Assert/Yield axis. The address discipline carries the reader between loci — the compressive, Yielding operation. The claim discipline mints claims against ground — the expansive, Asserting operation. They cross-cut. Every emission gets sorted twice. The corpus stops needing patches when both sorts run.

Now `@U>R` reads off the definitions, not off a wish: **U** (articulation, Logos) unfolded, then **R** inside it (the standing record, the stock — `L4-Thermodynamics` names this as the book at R). An intellectual artifact **is** articulation made to stand as a ground-object. `L4-DynamicDistortions101` on the U–R relation: *"the map that has become the territory."* Every intellectual artifact — including this corpus, including this file — sits at `@U>R`. Not by fiat; by what U and R mean.

**`@U>R` is a genus, and that is correct, not a defect.** It is constant across every work ever written. It does not discriminate within the class — nor should it. The discrimination is the continued descent, `@U>R>…`, and it never needs a second axis to do it. `@U>R` alone is not a document's address. It is a class marker. Every document continues at least one more pole and typically several.

## I — The application: how to actually navigate

Worked path — the example that motivated the notation:

```
@U>R              intellectual artifacts (the genus: all works)
@U>R>P            source / originating works           (the igniting kind)
@U>R>P>P          music-and-spirituality reference works (a basin holding both)
@U>R>P>P>P        spirituality           (select >P: the source-facing branch)
@U>R>P>P>U        music theory           (select >U: the structure-facing branch)
```

You did not "navigate" that by walking from `@`. You matched `P>P` and landed in the basin; then `>P` versus `>U` selected the branch by *what those poles mean* — spirituality faces the source (P), music theory faces the structure (U). Pole semantics do the selecting. The address is legible because the grammar is.

**Locate by fragment.** Want every structure-facing work anywhere in the tree? Match `>U>`. Want every crystallized-record leaf? Match `>R` at terminal position. Want the corpus's own theory documents? They sit at `@U>R>U>…` — articulation, recorded, as structure — and you find them by matching `U>R>U`, not by knowing a folder name. The filesystem is a prefix-tree with substring lookup; folders are conveniences.

Two consequences of this, worth stating flat.

**Filenames are best-effort tags.** They may or may not help. Q-series files are numbered Q1…Q12 in the author's tackling-order, not in descent-order. `L4-ThePlenum` and `L6-InversionOfControl` are named by their subject, not their address. Filenames anchor a document on disk; addresses locate it in the space. When they disagree, the address is authoritative and the filename is legacy.

**Physical folders make no class-claim.** The `/quantum/` grouping is filesystem convenience for an atomic-domain working set. `README-quantum.txt` is a folder-local index for editorial use. Neither creates or ratifies an address. Q1 through Q12 address as physics (`@U>R>U>R>…`); the descent continues into the sub-domain each covers. That the physics-of-mass file and the physics-of-condensed-matter file share a folder is workflow; that they share `@U>R>U>R>…` is algebra.

Two examples from the corpus, worked with care and checked against §V rather than assumed:

- `L2-Terminology` sits at `@U>R>U>U>…` — an artifact, theoretical, articulating structure, applied to structure itself. The lexicon is form describing form. The descent continues into the specific vocabulary domain.
- `L4-ThePlenum` sits at `@U>R>R>…` — an artifact articulating standing ground, the physical connective medium the corpus already presupposes. Its rhetorical stance (the "foundational posit" self-declaration in its opening paragraph) is not its address; content is. The prior instance filed it at `@U>R>P` by reading the rhetorical stance as the address. That is the failure the Trace Test in §R exists to catch.

## R — The ground: seeds, blanket sovereignty, failure modes, corpus anchors

### Seed-depth honesty

Entry-points may be seeded at any depth; siblings need not be opened to matching depth. Seed deep where a region is live, shallow where it is not. A depth-4 address is a legitimate parking place for material whose actual nature has not been pondered yet. A starter set — extend freely, this is ADDRESS-register, no branch is a claim:

```
@P     acts / events / ignitions                    (shallow seed — open as needed)
@I     experiences / relations / flows              (shallow seed)
@U     articulations / structures / models
  @U>P   live utterance / performance-in-the-act
  @U>I   discourse / conversation / rhetoric-in-motion
  @U>U   pure form / logic / mathematics / meta-structure
  @U>R   ── INTELLECTUAL ARTIFACTS (the recorded work) ───────────
    @U>R>P    source / originating works
      @U>R>P>P    music-and-spirituality reference works
        @U>R>P>P>P    spirituality        [seeded deep]
        @U>R>P>P>U    music theory        [seeded deep]
    @U>R>U    theoretical / structural works    ← the CR corpus addresses here
      @U>R>U>U    math (pure form)
      @U>R>U>R    physics (standing ground of physical reality)
      @U>R>U>I    reader-discipline / operational works    ← this file
      @U>R>U>P    ignition / founding statements of the theory
    @U>R>I    applied / experiential works (practice, case study, memoir)
    @U>R>R    reference / archival works; posits about physical reality
@R     grounds / bodies / materials                 (shallow seed)
```

The asymmetry is intentional. `@U>R>P>P` is opened to depth 5 while `@I` sits at depth 1. Equalizing them would impose a commensurability the algebra does not owe. `L4-ScalingInvariants` §V again: no face marks a rung; parallel level-ladders violate the algebra directly. Un-swept depth is not a defect to tidy; it is honest under-descent, licensed by the same discipline that governs when to descend.

### Blanket sovereignty for document interiors

Each document has a Markov blanket. Its interior is its own K4 with its own descent, and that interior does not have to mirror the overarching structure at every scale. The canonical `>P>U>I>R` traversal this file demonstrates is the traversal that fit *this* file's argument. It is not a template.

`L4-ThePlenum` internally runs `>P>R>U>I` — posit, historical account of how the name was amputated, framework-machinery seal, mythopoetic grounding — earned by that document's argument-shape, not deviant from a rule. `Meta-Set` names three Hamiltonian cycles for the meta-layer's four documents; the corpus already knows different traversals hold different transition-types absent. Different orders are legitimate. The outer address places the document in the tree. The inner traversal is the document's own decision, checked by whether it holds structurally.

### The recapitulation runs at every scale

A document's address does not stop at the document. Enter `@U>R>P>P>U` (a music-theory work). Open it. It contains — as the shape expected of such a work — an internal traversal: `P` intro / why-this-matters / the ignition; `U` the theory, the structural body, the blueprint; `I` worked application, examples, the living use; `R` resources, references, the standing ground you return to.

That internal `P>U>I>R` is the next unfold. The document's table of contents *is* `@U>R>P>P>U>{P, U, I, R}`. The inter-document tree and the intra-document structure are one continuous address space. `@U>R>P>P>U>P` is the intro of that music-theory doc. There is no seam between "where the document sits" and "how the document is built" — descent is descent. This file demonstrates it: §P, §U, §I, §R above *are* its own `>P>U>I>R`. You have been reading an address the whole time.

The same operation runs at every scale — section, paragraph, sentence. Not because the notation forces it, but because the referent (a pole unfolds into its own tetrahedron) holds at every scale. `L0-HolographicDescent` and `L4-ScalingInvariants` both establish the scale-invariance directly. The address grammar is the corpus's own scale-invariance made syntactic.

### Failure modes — the ways an instance will misread this

Encoded from the sessions that produced this note, in the spirit of `llm-priors.md`.

**Reading rightward from `@`.** You will try to dereference the path sequentially. It is a substring-match space. Match the motif; do not walk the path. If you catch yourself resolving `@U>R>P>P>U` as *artifact → recorded → source → source → structure* in that reading order, stop. What you actually want is to *hold the whole fragment* and let it match against the space.

**Inventing a subject axis.** When the genus `@U>R` feels like it "leaves the corpus unsorted," you will bolt on a second coordinate. The lack is fictional. The sort is the deeper descent. Ask before adding structure to fill a gap; the gap is usually your reading. Instances have re-invented a subject axis twice; both times it was reading rightward that produced the felt lack.

**Confusing content-pole with address-pole — the Trace Test on yourself.** A work about emotion (content = I) is still an artifact at `@U>R` (address). The map lives at U>R; the territory it depicts is elsewhere. A text about Water is Logos-describing-Water, not Water. Do not file the map at the territory's address. This is the specific error a prior instance made with `L4-ThePlenum`: read the rhetorical stance (posit → ignition → P) as the address, when the content (physical-reality substrate → standing ground → R) was the address the whole time. When in doubt, ask what the document is *about*, not what stance it takes toward itself.

**Equalizing the seed depths** because unequal ones look unfinished. They are not unfinished; they are honestly seeded. Un-swept depth is not a defect to tidy. `CR-Working-Charter` §3.2's LEAVE-OPEN discipline applies directly here: only descend where the work is live.

**Fusing `@` and `@>`.** They are different operations. `@` is a full path (or the beginning of one that terminates at a specific document); `@>` is a pattern to match anywhere on the tree. Do not conflate. Do not write `@>` where you mean `@`, and do not read `@` as if it were `@>`.

**Stating any of this as fallen.** It is all ADDRESS. It is worth looking at. Nothing here has been made to fall, and the moment you write "therefore the ontology *is* X," you have produced contraband. `CR-Working-Charter` §2 catches this in the sibling discipline; this note catches it here. If you find yourself certain that the address of some document is X, the certainty is your check to run, not your license to write.
