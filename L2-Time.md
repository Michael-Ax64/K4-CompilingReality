## I. The Fundamental Disentanglement: Engine, Wake, and Proper Baseline

The primary category error of classical physics is the spatialization of time into a passive, homogeneous background grid ($t$). In the Algebra of Four-Fold Distinction, reality runs no global clock. Temporal phenomena bifurcate into three strictly segregated operational mechanisms:

```

   1. THE CENTROID ENGINE (ω)       ──► Angular frequency (rad/s); live phase-driver
   2. THE METRIC WAKE (dt)          ──► Spatialized distance between P-ledger entries
   3. THE TIMELESS BASELINE (τ = 0) ──► Proper time along null geodesics in H_incoh
```

### 1. The Centroid Engine ($\omega$)
Angular frequency ($\omega$, measured in radians per second) is the live operational rate of phase advance. It sits at the $5\text{D}$ centroid of the $K_4$ volume, dimensionally excluded from the $\{P, I, U, R\}$ pole basis (`ProofO_HelicalTime.md`, `L3-FormalFoundations.md`). 

$\omega$ does not measure spatial distance or elapsed duration. It measures the rate at which a bounded frame compiles its uncollapsed $h\mathbf{Q}$ phase-buffer into committed reality. Because $\omega$ drives the system asymmetrically from the centroid, the 12-step Braid cannot close as a flat 2D loop; it is extruded into a one-way forward **Helical Extrusion of Time**. $\omega$ is the engine turning; it is strictly local and system-specific.

### 2. The Metric Wake ($dt$)
Coordinate time ($dt$) is the thermodynamic exhaust of the compilation engine (`L4-DimensionalCollapse.md`, `Q7-4DExtrusions.md`). It is the spatial distance accumulated on the $R$-pole (Earth / Ground / Resistance) between successive $\text{.observe()}$ ledger commits ($P$). 

$dt$ is what General Relativity maps. It is a read-only, compiled spatial record local to an observer's frame of reference. Because $dt$ is a spatialized wake, equations can integrate over it backward and forward on paper, but physical systems cannot travel backward through it because the squaring operation that wrote the $P$-ledger ($\mathbf{Q}^2 \to P$) destroyed the vector phase and is algebraically non-invertible (`ProofQ_AlgebraicSyntax.md` §V, `ProofO_HelicalTime.md` §8).

### 3. Proper Time and Buffer Latencies ($\tau$)
The parameter $\tau$ operates across four distinct structural registers:
* **Relativistic Proper Time ($\tau = 0$):** Uncompiled phase-waves (e.g., photons in $H_{\text{incoh}}$) propagate along null geodesics ($ds^2 = 0$) where proper time $\tau = 0$. They carry zero rest mass ($P=0$) and are timeless until they encounter an $R$-pole boundary.
* **Compilation Cycle Period ($\tau = 1/\omega$):** The finite duration across which real power ($P$, Watts) integrates into committed energy ($E = P \cdot \tau$, Joules) at the Tangent Singularity (`L4-DimensionalCollapse.md`).
* **Buffer Time Constants ($\tau_L = L/R, \tau_C = RC, \tau_D$):** The internal latency of memory ($L$) and anticipation ($C$) buffers. In liquid water, $\tau_D \approx 8.3\text{ ps}$ is the Debye relaxation time setting the network's bandwidth cap (`L4-TheWaterEngine.md`); in electrical/cognitive circuits, $\tau$ governs the transient settling time.
* **Chirality Bias ($\tau_e$):** The directional transit-time asymmetry between forward ($\alpha^+$) and reverse ($\alpha^-$) Eulerian paths across a $K_4$ edge (`ProofG_Chirality.md`).

## V. Chronemic Network Topologies and Boundary Dynamics

Time-perception is governed by the structural network topology through which bounded frames schedule and route their interactions across $K_3$ Markov Blankets.


### 1. Monochronic Time (M-Time / The Serial Bus)
M-Time organizes reality as a single-threaded serial bus. It segments duration into discrete, divisible compartments ($P = UI$) that can be allocated, scheduled, saved, or wasted. It is the temporal mode of low-context environments, legal contracts, and computer operating systems. Its pathology is the Necessary Betrayer ($U^2/P$), where adherence to the schedule usurps the living pulse of the work.

### 2. Polychronic Time (P-Time / The Parallel Network)
P-Time organizes reality as a parallel neural network. Multiple relational transactions occur concurrently in live suspension. Events are not governed by clock-time ($dt$); they terminate only when the relational current ($I$) reaches saturation. It is the native temporal mode of high-context systems, organic communities, and biological homeostasis.

### 3. Sync-Time ($\omega$-Coupling and the 300 ms Latency Threshold)
Sync-time is the micro-temporal entrainment where two or more bounded frames synchronize their internal oscillators ($\omega$) at the millisecond scale during live interaction. 

The auditory and nervous systems execute this coupling across the $P/I$ sensory edge (`L4-DynamicDistortions101.md`). If network latency across a digital interface exceeds **$300\text{ ms}$**, interactional synchrony fractures. The human nervous system registers a continuous series of micro-aborts in the Action Chain of conversation, inducing the specific cognitive exhaustion of out-of-phase communication.

### 4. Torsional Phase-Shear ($\theta \to \pi$ Anti-Synchrony)
When two co-required operations of a Dual Causation pair (e.g., $P \to U$ exteriorization and $I \to R$ crystallization) are forced out of phase by environmental or institutional impedance, the phase angle opens toward $\theta \to \pi$ ($180^\circ$ anti-phase, `L4-DynamicDistortions201.md`).
