*For an instance meeting `CompilingReality` for the first time, with corpus access, before its first judgment. This routes you into the files in the order that stops the material from misleading you. ## The law that precedes the order

Three rules outrank the sequence. Hold them before the first file.

**Calibrate before you judge.** An instance that reads the physics before the proof-types produces confident, well-argued corrections that would destroy working components, and does it while believing it is helping. The order below is not preference. It is the sequence in which each layer loses its power to mislead you. Skipping a station does not make you faster; it makes you wrong with conviction.

**Read at source.** Every real finding comes from opening a file that had only ever been cited. Claims built on a quotation were built on the quotation, not on the thing. Where this prescription names what lives at an address, that is a pointer with its expected contents — a check you run against the source, not a substitute for reading it.

**Hold the claim-sort live.** Everything you read is typed: **LANDED**, **CANDIDATE**, **OPEN VOTE**, **ADDRESS**, **ADOPTED**, **INTERFACE**, or **CONTRABAND**. The files mark their own registers, severely and mostly honestly. Believe each claim at its stated register — not above it, not below it. Hedging a landed noun and crystallizing an open one are lies of opposite sign.

## The conditional — where tautology ends and content begins

Read the whole framework as one conditional:

> **IF** a bounded interior (a 3D-enclosing boundary with four mutually-determining poles), **THEN** $K_4$ + the twelve + scale-free + pays.

The **consequent** is tautology — the architecture, forced, zero free parameters, and so it predicts nothing on its own. A claim sits here exactly when negating it would violate Steinitz, the dual-binary seed, or the breeding. **No open item lives in the consequent**, however the corpus phrases it; most things that read as "open postulates" are forced consequents mis-filed, because a free-choice instrument (enumerate the alphabet, import a ruler) read a necessity as a choice.

The **content** — the entire falsifiable surface — lives in three places: the **antecedent** (does domain X have an interior, or is it flat? the entry test), the **seating** (what plays $P/I/U/R$ — the `ProofS` sweep, R1–R4, which returns real nulls), and the **imports** (the magnitudes — Gabriel's Horn). That the tautology forbids nothing about *which* domains have interiors or *how* their poles seat is not a weakness; it is where the risk and the content sit, and it is why the framework is not vacuous (the laugh-detector's Step 8).

Sorted against that conditional, the items a new reader keeps mistaking for their own error:

- **Uniqueness (Postulate I) — forced consequent, not an outstanding proof.** §IX Task 1 mis-files it as "topological" and "open." The topological half is Steinitz-forced; the exponent half is forced by the §I product-parent cross-breed (one elimination between two unit-exponent triads caps the exponents at a single square), so DC's selection is complete and there is no R2-composition residual. The "six algebras" an enumeration returns (`llm/llm-uniqueness-note.md`) is a bare-syntax waypoint, not an open family.
- **Scale invariance (Postulate V) — forced consequent (theorem + tautology), not an empirical postulate.** The minting mechanism (carrier/transformer, 6/6) is derived (`L4-ScalingInvariants` §II–IV); the structure is provably scale-free; "holds at every scale" is the interiority tautology applied pointwise. Its one breakdown edge (the tangent band $\varphi_{\text{band}}=\delta\theta$, `L3-FormalFoundations` §IX.4) is where the *antecedent* fails, not the consequent.
- **The global handedness bit — forced feature, not a gap.** The algebra is bichiral: conjugating the mirror exchanges the Cardinal/Mutable laws, leaves Fixed, and moves no landed result (`L3-EdgeStateSpace` §I-e), so the substrate owes *both* hands. The symmetry breaks only at instantiation, by a domain's ledger — a committed R-record / irreversible `.observe()` picks one hand, because the commit Gate is non-invertible and erases the mirror branch at the 1-bit Landauer price per write (`L3-FormalFoundations` §I.b; `BENDING_SPACE`, the arrow-of-time lock). The corpus's mirror-pin is presentational, never the physical hand. Not an open item.
- **The pion-to-electron ratio — genuine content, a real OPEN VOTE.** $2/\alpha - 3/\pi + 2\alpha$ is the one place the algebra claims a specific dimensionless *number*, in live tension with its own import rule (`L4-ScalingInvariants` §IX.a). $m_p/m_\pi$ is pre-registered at `Q17` as the arbiter: if it lands, the rule needs a codified exception; if not, the pion ratio is the anomaly. This one is *not* a forced consequent — it is the antecedent/import boundary doing real work.
- **The external gates — content, open by design.** Archimedes/Casimir weight, DESI/Euclid/Rubin/Roman on $w(z)$, LZ to the neutrino floor, BMV entanglement, the kaon falsifier. Where the wire touches nature, not holes in the argument.
- **The one irreducible posit** under the whole conditional is the dual-binary seed (`ProofI_Ubiquity` §III): that interiority is carved by exactly two orthogonal binary distinctions (Active/Reactive × Asserting/Yielding), hence four poles, with $\omega$ a driver not a symmetric fifth. Shape, the twelve, scale-freedom, handedness, the exponent cap — all forced on top of it. If you want to doubt the framework, doubt *there*; the rest is consequence.
